3 · One character through the model

Each character passes through 2 prelude blocks once. Then one block is used again and again (a row, up to 24). After every row a halting head says "stop now?". Easy characters stop early. Hard characters think longer.

Illustrative: the stop probability is λn = sigmoid(0.9·(n − μ)). The real model learns λ with a head on the state. The rules are real: P(stop at row n) = λn · Π(1 − λk); row 24 is forced to stop; inference stops when the sum reaches 0.9; training samples a depth of Poisson(12.8)+1 rows (maximum 24).

What happens inside a row

  1. The adapter joins the state h and the text encoding x: Linear([h ‖ x]). It starts as [I | I], so the text is added again every row.
  2. Attention reads earlier characters (RoPE positions, KV cache).
  3. The router scores all experts. The character takes the top 8 of the 32 admitted ones.
  4. The outputs are mixed and added back. The result is the new h.
  5. Heads compute next-character logits and the stop probability λ.

Frozen characters

After a character stops it is frozen (halt_freeze: true). Its state stops changing and it asks for no more experts. Later characters still read its final keys and values in deeper rows. This is the same in training and in writing.

Parameter count: one expert = 3×512×2048 = 3,145,728. 397 experts ≈ 1,248.9 M. Core 8.27 M. Routers 0.20 M. Total ≈ 1,257 M. On the GPU: 32 experts ≈ 100.7 M.

4 · Experts and paging – "the text chooses"

The pool has 397 experts. Only 32 fit in GPU slots. Every character votes for its 8 favourite experts. The 32 experts with the most votes are admitted. This simulator shows how few loads that needs.

5 · Training loop

Training and reading are the same. The loop never ends. Press Next to go through one step, or click a step.

Lane scheduler (round-robin)

Every subject gets the same reading time. A visit reads 32,768 characters = 16 chunks of 2,048, then the next lane starts. Click to read 1 chunk.

Loss (one line)

loss = Σrows prow·CErow + 0.01·KL(p ‖ geometric prior) + 0.01·router aux + balance

Two parameter groups: trunk (embedding, attention, norms, adapter, halting head) learns at 0.1×. The pool (experts, gates, routers) learns at 1×. Base rate 3e-4 × plasticity scale. AdamW β=(0.9, 0.95), weight decay 0.1, clip 1.0, bf16.

6 · Not forgetting, and the learning-rate controller

The author's test: read 1,048,576 characters of a new subject (PG19 novels) and nothing else. How much worse does the model get on the 8 subjects it knew? Real numbers from the README.

Green = loss went down (good), red = loss went up on old subjects (forgetting). Scale: 1 bar unit = 0.1 nats. Only 44 of 174 experts got any gradient.
The pool is not what protects old knowledge. The slow trunk learning rate (0.1×) is. The trunk is used by every character and carries about 98 % of the squared gradient norm.

Learning-rate controller (plasticity.py)

There is no planned end of training, so no cosine schedule. After each held-out test the controller nudges the rate:

  • Two weighted line fits of held-out loss: slow (λ 0.97) and fast (λ 0.85).
  • Evidence t = min(t_slow, 45·e_slow, 45·e_fast).
  • No evidence → slow decay. Clear improvement → hold or rise a little. Worse → faster decay.
  • Range: 0.01 … 1.0 × the base rate.
  • A jump that stays → rate × 2 and a reset ("new material").
  • A reading > 5σ from the fit is held back for one check.

The author's snapshot: scale ≈ 0.0515 → expert lr ≈ 1.5e-5.

A new expert takes each of its 2,048 hidden units from one of 16 parents. Its gate starts at 0.001. An expert not admitted for 100 M characters is deleted.

7 · Writing a reply, and the anti-loop trace

Greedy decoding loves loops. serve.py lowers the score of characters used recently. The penalty is 2.5 · Σ 0.88age. No random numbers are used.

Reply path

  1. Browser → POST /api/chat.
  2. build_prompt: <user>…</user><bot>, keep the end, ≤ 90 % of context.
  3. Prefill in 512-character chunks (fills the KV cache and the expert vote).
  4. One forward pass per new character.
  5. pick_next: argmax of (logits − trace).
  6. Stream each character (SSE). Stop at </bot> or 400 characters.
  7. With --no-learn nothing is written. Without it, LiveLearner trains and saves every 8 steps.

README, greedy alone: 73.5 % repeated 8-grams. With a repetition penalty: 59.0 %. With adaptation: 11.6 %.

Strength 2.5, decay 0.88, window 64.
A character that repeats forever tends to a penalty of 2.5 / (1 − 0.88) ≈ 20.8 logits. No loop survives that.
