REGULATION XV

GAMBLING AND LOTTERIES

Gambling and lotteries are subject to state law (California Penal Code, Sections 330 through 337(s)) and therefore are not permitted on campus at University functions or by University organizations.

Gambling includes, among other things, any percentage game played with cards, dice, or any other device for money, checks, credit, or anything else representative of value. Those who deal, play, carry out, or conduct such games, or place bets in connection with such games, are guilty of a misdemeanor and subject to a fine, imprisonment, University disciplinary action, or any combination of sanctions.
