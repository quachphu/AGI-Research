Policy for Certain Named Endowments at CSULB California State University, Long Beach recognizes that endowments provide an important role in achieving the educational goals of the institution as expressed in the University's Mission Statement, which recognizes teaching, scholarly and creative activity, and community service as the faculty's three major responsibilities.
