## Departmentalization of International Studies Program

California State University, Long Beach Policy Statement

Policy Number: 21-04

Date: March 16, 2021

## DEPARTMENTALIZATION OF INTERNATIONAL

## STUDIES PROGRAM

Per the procedure outlined in CSULB Policy Statement 95-19, the College of Liberal Arts has taken appropriate steps in proposing the departmentalization of the International Studies Program.

With College of Liberal Arts Faculty Council approval (10/28/20), Academic Senate approval (2/25/21) and Presidential concurrence (3/10/21), the departmentalization will take effect Fall 2021.
