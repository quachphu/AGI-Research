## Policy Statement - 70-13 LeafLetting

Minutes 5/15/70

## POLICY STATEMENT APPROVED AS-70-13

## LEAFLETTING

Leafletting, exclusive of commercial advertising and not in violation of the California Penal Code, is permitted in any area of the campus outside of buildings and exclusive of the parking lots.

Revision of Statement

Approved by the Academic Senate 4/24/70.

Approved by the President of the College 5/1/70.
