## Policy Statement - 13-02 GPA Requirements, Undergraduate

## UNDERGRADUATE GRADE POINT AVERAGE REQUIREMENTS

(This Policy Statement supersedes PS 94-01 and PS 86-08)

This policy was recommended by the Academic Senate on February 7, 2013

and received the concurrence of the President on February 15, 2013.

The grade point average standards of Section 40402 of Title 5 of the California Administrative Code requires undergraduate students, at graduation, to have completed the following with a grade point average of at least 2.0:

1. All units applicable toward a degree, including those accepted by transfer from another institution,

2. All units in the major, and

3. All units attempted at the campus granting the degree.

EFFECTIVE: Immediately
