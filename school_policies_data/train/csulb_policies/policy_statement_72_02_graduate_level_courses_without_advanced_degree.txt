## Policy Statement - 72-02 Graduate Level Courses without Advanced Degree

Minutes 9/24/71

## AS (GC) 72-2

## POLICY STATEMENT APPROVED

## GRADUATE LEVEL COURSES WITHOUT ADVANCED DEGREE

If a department is not currently authorized to offer an advanced degree, approval to offer 500-600 level courses must be received from the Graduate Council.

Distributed to the Academic Senate 4/16/71 and 4/30/71.

Deemed approved by the Academic Senate 5/14/71.

Approved by the President of the College 6/1/71.
