## College Title Change: College of Professional and Continuing Education (CPaCE)

California State University, Long Beach Policy Statement 22-08

April 11, 2022

College Title Change: College of Professional and Continuing Education (CPaCE)

(The policy was recommended by the Academic Senate on March 24, 2022 and approved by the President on April 7, 2022.)

The College of Professional and International Education (CPIE) is changing its name to the College of Professional and Continuing Education (CPaCE).

EFFECTIVE: Immediately
