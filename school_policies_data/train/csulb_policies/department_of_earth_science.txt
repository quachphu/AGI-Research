## Department of Earth Science

California State University, Long Beach Policy Statement 22-27

November 14, 2022

Department of Earth Science

(This department name change was approved the Academic Senate on October 27, 2022 and approved by the Provost on November 14, 2022.)

Effective Fall 2023, the Department of Geological Sciences will update its name to: the Department of Earth Science.

EFFECTIVE: Fall 2023
