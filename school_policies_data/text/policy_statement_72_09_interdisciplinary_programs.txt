## Policy Statement - 72-09 Interdisciplinary Programs

## POLICY STATEMENT APPROVED

Minutes 12/10/71

## AS (PEP) 72-9

## INTERDISCIPLINARY PROGRAMS

Any interdisciplinary program proposal ( certificate or otherwise) which involves disciplines from two or more schools shall utilize the same procedures for curriculum approval and financial support a s required of all other curriculum program proposals involving two or more schools. Interdisciplinary programs falling entirely within the jurisdiction of a single school shall utilize the same procedures for curriculum approval and financial support as required of all other curriculum program proposals falling within a single school.

Distributed to the Academic Senate 10/15/71 and 11/5/71.

Deemed approved by the Academic Senate 11/5/71.

Approved by the President of the College 12/1/71.
