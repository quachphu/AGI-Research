## Department of Comparative World Literature and Classics

California State University, Long Beach

Policy Statement 25-19
July 23, 2025

Department of Comparative World Literature and Classics

(This new department title was approved by the CSULB Academic Senate on May 1, 2025, and approved by the CSULB Provost on May 8, 2025.)

The following areas will merge into one department:
- Classics
- Comparative World Literature

The new department title will be: Comparative World Literature and Classics

EFFECTIVE: Fall 2025
