## Policy Statement - 73-20 CR/NC Ruling on English 300 and/or 317, Revision of

Minutes 4/27/73

## POLICY STATEMENT APPROVED

## AS(GC)73-20

## REVISION CR/NC RULING ON ENGLISH 300 AND/OR 317

Post-baccalaureate students have the option of enrolling for Credit-No Credit (C level proficiency) .in English 300 and/or 317. Units completed on this basis shall not be included among those required for a credential or post-baccalaureate degree.

Approved by Graduate Council 2'/21/73, Minutes. Approved 3/7/73.

Distributed to Academic Senate 3/23/73 and 4/6/73.

Deemed Approved by the Academic Senate 4/6/73.

Approved by the President of the University 4/19/73.
