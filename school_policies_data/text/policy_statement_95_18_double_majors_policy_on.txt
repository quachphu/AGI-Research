## Policy Statement - 95-18 Double Majors, Policy on

Policy on Double Major

This Policy was recommended by the Academic Senate on November 9, 1995 and

Approved by the President on November 27, 1995

Two baccalaureate programs may be completed concurrently. Only one degree will be conferred and only one diploma issued. The fact that the requirements of another program have been completed will be noted on the transcript. A course, or courses, may be used to satisfy the individual requirements of both majors, without limit, as long as the required pattern of coursework is completed for each major.

EFFECTIVE: Immediately
