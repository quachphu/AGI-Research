## Department of Cinematic Arts

California State University, Long Beach Policy Statement 23-21

October 27, 2023

Department of Cinematic Arts

(This new department title was approved by the CSULB Academic Senate on October 19, 2023, and approved by the CSULB Provost on October 26, 2023)

The Department of Film and Electronic Arts will alter its name to the Department of Cinematic Arts.

EFFECTIVE: Fall 2024

College: 55, College of the Arts
