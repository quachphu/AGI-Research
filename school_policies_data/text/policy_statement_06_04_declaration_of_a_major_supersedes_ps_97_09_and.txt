## Policy Statement - 06-04 Declaration of a Major (Supersedes PS 97-09 and 02-08)

Policy Statement

06-04

June 2, 2006

Declaration of a Major

(This supersedes policy statement 02-08)

This policy was recommended by the Senate on March 15, 2006and approved by the President on March 23, 2006.

To help ensure timely completion of graduation requirements, students who have a total of 60 units completed must have declared a major before they may register for the next term. Upper-division transfer students are required to declare their intended major on their application for admission.

## EFFECTIVE: 2006
