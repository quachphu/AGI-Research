## MASTER AGREEMENT BETWEEN CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY, LONG
BEACH AND CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY, LONG BEACH FOUNDATION
FOR ADMINISTRATION OF GRANTS AND CONTRACTS
An Operating Agreement between the Trustees and California State University, Long Beach University (University) and California State University, Long Beach Foundation (Auxiliary), effective March 1, 2002 through February 28, 2007, authorizes the Auxiliary to perform the function "Externally Funded Projects Including Research, Workshops, Conferences and Institutes," as specified in Section 42500, Title 5, California Code of Regulations.

The Auxiliary agrees to indemnify, defend and save harmless the University from all loss, damage or liability that may be suffered or incurred by the University caused by, arising out of, or in any way connected with the administration of Sponsored Programs by the Auxiliary; and further agrees to carry adequate insurance in accordance with California State University Policy, as specified by Executive Order Nos. 890 and 849.
This Master Agreement effective July 1, 2004 through February 28, 2007, designates Auxiliary as the Sponsored Program Administrator (SPA) by University, except for those awards which the University's Provost and Senior Vice President for Academic Affairs or designee, and Vice President for Administration and Finance or designee, and the Executive Director of the Foundation or designee mutually agree the University shall be the SPA.

Auxiliary, when acting as SPA, agrees to comply with CSU Executive Order No. 890 and to provide grants and contracts services to ensure the submission of proposals and administration of contracts and grants in accordance with CSU Long Beach's policy for Sponsored Program Administration, federal and state regulations, and other applicable California State University, campus and Auxiliary policies.
