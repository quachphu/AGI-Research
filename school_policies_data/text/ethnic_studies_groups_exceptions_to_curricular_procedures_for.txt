## EXCEPTIONS TO CURRICULAR PROCEDURES FOR ETHNIC STUDIES GROUPS
Deemed approved by the Academic Senate 12/12/69, Approved by the President of the College 2/24/70.

Wherever any exception to normal curricular procedures is authorized for one ethnic studies group, the same exception rill automatically be granted to other ethnic studies groups.
Distributed to the Academic Senate 11/14/69 and 12/12/69.
