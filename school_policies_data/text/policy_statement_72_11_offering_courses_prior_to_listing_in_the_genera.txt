## POLICY STATEMENT APPROVED

## AS (PEP) 72-11

## POLICY ON OFFERING COURSES PRIOR TO LISTING IN

## THE GENERAL COLLEGE BULLETIN

The policy of the College is that regular courses (not experimental) should ordinarily be listed in the General College Bulletin for the year in which they are to be offered. However, an exception may be made for a course which has received all necessary curricular approvals up to and including certification by Academic Planning for inclusion in the next General Bulletin. Such a course may be authorized for offering with approval of the Dean of the School concerned.

Distributed to Academic Senate 10/15/71 and 11/5/71.
