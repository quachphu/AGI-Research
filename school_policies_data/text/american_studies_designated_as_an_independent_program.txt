## American Studies Designated as an Independent Program

California State University, Long Beach Policy Statement

Policy Number: 21-11

Date: May 24, 2021

American Studies

Designated as an Independent Program

With Academic Senate approval (5/17/21) and Presidential concurrence (5/17/21), American Studies is now designated as an independent program in the College of the Liberal Arts.

Effective: Fall 2021
