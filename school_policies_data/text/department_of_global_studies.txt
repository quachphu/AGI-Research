## Department of Global Studies

California State University, Long Beach

Policy Statement 25-06

February 20, 2025

Department of Global Studies

(This updated department title was approved by the CSULB Academic Senate on February 6, 2025, and approved by the President on February 7, 2025.)

The Department of International Studies will update its title to the Department of Global Studies.

An update to the course prefix will accompany this change.

EFFECTIVE: Fall 2025

College: College of Liberal Arts
