California State University, Long Beach

Policy Statement 70-13 May 15, 1970

## LEAFLETTING
This policy was approved by the Academic Senate April 24, 1970 and approved by the President of the College on May 1, 1970.
Leafletting, exclusive of commercial advertising and not in violation of the California Penal Code, is permitted in any area of the campus outside of buildings and exclusive of the parking lots.
