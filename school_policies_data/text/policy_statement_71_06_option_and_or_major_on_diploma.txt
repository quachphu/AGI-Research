## Policy Statement - 71-06 Option and/or Major on Diploma

Minutes 1/15/71

## POLICY STATEMENT APPROVED AS (PEP) 71-6

## OPTION AND/OR MAJOR ON DIPLOMA

Whenever a student has completed a college-approved option within a major, that option shall be indicated on his diploma along with his major, except where his major department has voted to reject this policy with all of its majors. Should any department vote to reject this policy, that department must convey this information to the Dean of its School and Records Office.

Deemed Approved by the Academic Senate 12/11/70.

Approved by the President of the College, 1/6/71.
