## Policy Statement - 70-07 Ethnic Studies Groups, Exceptions to Curricular Procedures for

Minutes 2/27/70

## POLICY STATEMENT APPROVED AS(PEP)70-7

## EXCEPTIONS TO CURRICULAR PROCEDURES FOR ETHNIC STUDIES GROUPS

Wherever any exception to normal curricular procedures is authorized for one ethnic studies group, the same exception will automatically be granted to other ethnic studies groups.

Distributed to the Academic Senate 11/14/69 and 12/12/69.

Deemed approved by the Academic Senate 12/12/69,

Approved by the President of the College 2/24/70.
