SUBJECT: Priorities For Scheduling Of Classroom Facilities

The following policy statement, recommended by the Academic Senate at its meeting of March 10, 1977, and approved by the President on April 14, 1977, is as follows:
Scheduling of classroom facilities be done in accordance with the following priorities:
1. Scheduled classes and related activities;
2. Continuing Education;
3. Other use (only if not needed for the purposes above).
