## Policy Statement - 94-13 Freshmen Enrollment in Upper Division Classes

Freshmen Enrollment in Upper Division Classes

Undergraduate students who have completed fewer than 30 units may not enroll in an upper division course without permission of the instructor.

Effective: Fall 1995
