### Approach
The key insight is to recognize that zombies will vote based on their optimal strategy, prioritizing survival and maximizing their brain intake. Heidi needs to secure enough votes to pass her proposal. The number of votes required is at least half of the attendees, which can be calculated using integer division. The minimum number of brains required is derived from the formula `(N + 1) // 2`, where `N` is the number of attendees. This formula ensures Heidi can secure the necessary votes by distributing the minimum number of brains while guaranteeing her survival and at least one brain for herself.

```python

```

1. **Understanding the Voting Mechanism**: Heidi's proposal needs at least half of the votes (including her own) to pass. This means she needs `ceil(N/2)` votes, which simplifies to `(N + 1) // 2` using integer division.
2. **Optimal Brain Distribution**: By distributing 1 brain to each of `(N + 1) // 2 - 1` zombies and keeping 1 for herself, Heidi ensures that those zombies will vote in favor of her proposal. This distribution is optimal because each of those zombies would otherwise risk getting nothing if the proposal fails and subsequent rounds occur.
3. **Efficiency**: The formula directly computes the result in constant time, making the solution efficient even for the upper limit of the input constraint (up to \(10^9\)).

This approach ensures that Heidi can always secure the necessary votes with the minimum number of brains, leveraging the mathematical properties of integer division to simplify the problem-solving process.
