### Approach
1. **First Occurrence Order**: Identify the order in which each book is first accessed. This helps in determining the initial arrangement of the books such that the lifting effort is minimized.
2. **First Part Sum Calculation**: Calculate the total weight of books lifted during their first access. This is done by summing the weights of all books that were accessed before the current book in the first occurrence order.
3. **Second Part Sum Calculation**: Calculate the total weight of books lifted during subsequent accesses. This involves summing the weights of distinct books accessed between consecutive accesses of each book.

n, m = map(int, input().split())
w = list(map(int, input().split()))
b = list(map(int, input().split()))

        seen.add(book)
        first_occurrence_order.append(book)

    current_book = first_occurrence_order[i]
