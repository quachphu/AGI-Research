To solve this problem, we need to determine the minimum cost to prepare the required number of A-pizzas and B-pizzas using a combination of purchasing A-pizzas, B-pizzas, and AB-pizzas. The key is to leverage the cost efficiency of AB-pizzas where possible, as they can be rearranged into A and B-pizzas when bought in pairs.

### Approach
1. **Understand the Options**: We can buy A-pizzas, B-pizzas, or AB-pizzas. Each AB-pizza pair (2 AB-pizzas) can be rearranged into 1 A-pizza and 1 B-pizza.
2. **Cost Comparison**: Compare the cost of buying AB-pizza pairs versus buying individual A and B-pizzas. If 2 * C (cost of two AB-pizzas) is cheaper than A + B (cost of one A and one B-pizza), it is more cost-effective to use AB-pizzas.
3. **Iterate Over Possible AB-pair Counts**: For each possible number of AB-pairs (K) from 0 to the maximum of X and Y, compute the total cost. This includes the cost of K AB-pairs plus the cost of any additional A or B-pizzas needed beyond what the AB-pairs provide.

### Solution Code
```python
A, B, C, X, Y = map(int, input().split())
max_xy = max(X, Y)
min_cost = float('inf')

for K in range(0, max_xy + 1):
    cost = 2 * K * C + max(0, X - K) * A + max(0, Y - K) * B
    if cost < min_cost:
        min_cost = cost

print(min_cost)
```

### Explanation
1. **Input Reading**: The input values are read and parsed into integers representing the costs of A, B, and AB-pizzas, and the required quantities X and Y.
2. **Determine Range for K**: The maximum value of K is determined as the maximum of X and Y to cover all possible scenarios where AB-pairs might be used.
3. **Cost Calculation**: For each K from 0 to the maximum of X and Y, compute the total cost. This includes the cost of K AB-pairs (each pair contributes 1 A and 1 B) and the cost of any additional A or B-pizzas needed beyond the contribution of the AB-pairs.
4. **Track Minimum Cost**: The minimum cost across all possible values of K is tracked and printed as the result.

This approach ensures that we explore all potential ways to use AB-pairs efficiently while covering the required number of A and B-pizzas, thus minimizing the total cost.
