Each friend has a restriction on numbers they can receive based on divisibility by given prime numbers. The goal is to find the smallest \( v \) that satisfies these constraints.

### Approach
1. **Problem Analysis**:
   - The first friend cannot receive numbers divisible by \( x \).
   - The second friend cannot receive numbers divisible by \( y \).
   - All numbers given to both friends must be distinct.
   - We need to ensure that the numbers given to each friend meet their respective constraints and that the total count of numbers given matches the required counts for both friends.

2. **Key Insights**:
   - Use binary search to efficiently determine the minimum \( v \).
   - For each candidate \( v \), calculate the total available numbers that can be allocated to both friends after excluding numbers that violate their constraints.
   - Check if the available numbers can satisfy the required counts for both friends.

3. **Algorithm**:
   - **Binary Search**: We perform binary search on \( v \) to find the minimum value that meets the constraints.
   - **Mathematical Calculations**:
     - Calculate the numbers divisible by both \( x \) and \( y \) (LCM of \( x \) and \( y \)).
     - Determine the numbers available for each friend after excluding the invalid numbers.
     - Check if the remaining numbers can satisfy the required counts.

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    import sys
    cnt1, cnt2, x, y = map(int, sys.stdin.readline().split())
    lcm_xy = x * y  # Since x and y are primes, LCM is x*y
    low = 1
    high = 2 * (10**18)  # A sufficiently large upper bound

    while low < high:
        mid = (low + high) // 2
        total_available = mid - (mid // lcm_xy)
        
        if (cnt1 + cnt2) > total_available:
            low = mid + 1
            continue
        
        # Calculate B and C
        div_x = mid // x
        div_y = mid // y
        div_xy = mid // lcm_xy
        B = div_x - div_xy
        C = div_y - div_xy
        
        needed_A1 = max(0, cnt1 - C)
        needed_A2 = max(0, cnt2 - B)
        needed_A = needed_A1 + needed_A2
        
        A = total_available - B - C
        
        if needed_A <= A:
            high = mid
        else:
            low = mid + 1
    
    print(low)

main()
```

### Explanation
1. **Binary Search**:
   - We start with a lower bound of 1 and an upper bound of \( 2 \times 10^{18} \).
   - For each midpoint \( mid \) in the binary search, we check if \( mid \) can be the minimum \( v \).

2. **Total Available Numbers**:
   - Calculate the total numbers available after excluding those divisible by both \( x \) and \( y \).

3. **Available Numbers for Each Friend**:
   - **B**: Numbers divisible by \( x \) but not \( y \).
   - **C**: Numbers divisible by \( y \) but not \( x \).
   - **A**: Numbers not divisible by either \( x \) or \( y \).

4. **Check Feasibility**:
   - Ensure the sum of required numbers does not exceed the total available.
   - Check if the remaining numbers (A) can cover the deficit after allocating B and C to the respective friends.

This approach efficiently narrows down the possible values of \( v \) using binary search, ensuring we find the minimum valid \( v \) in logarithmic time relative to the search range.
