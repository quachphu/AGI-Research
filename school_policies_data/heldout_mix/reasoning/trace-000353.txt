To solve this problem, we need to distribute the minimum number of tablets to patients such that each patient gets at least one tablet and patients with higher health scores than their immediate neighbors receive more tablets. The goal is to minimize the total number of tablets distributed.

### Approach
1. **Problem Analysis**:
   - Each patient must receive at least one tablet.
   - A patient with a higher health score than their immediate neighbor(s) must receive more tablets than those neighbors.
   - We need to ensure the solution is efficient given the constraint of up to 100,000 patients.

2. **Key Insight**:
   - Use two passes over the list of health scores to determine the minimum tablets required:
     - **Left to Right Pass**: Ensures each patient gets more tablets than their left neighbor if their health score is higher.
     - **Right to Left Pass**: Ensures each patient gets more tablets than their right neighbor if their health score is higher.
   - For each patient, the final number of tablets is the maximum value obtained from the two passes.

3. **Algorithm**:
   - **Left Pass**: Traverse from left to right, incrementing tablets for each patient if their health score is higher than the left neighbor.
   - **Right Pass**: Traverse from right to left, incrementing tablets for each patient if their health score is higher than the right neighbor.
   - Sum the maximum values from both passes for each patient to get the minimum total tablets.

### Solution Code
```python
import sys

def main():
    n = int(sys.stdin.readline())
    ratings = [int(sys.stdin.readline()) for _ in range(n)]
    
    if n == 0:
        print(0)
        return
    
    left = [1] * n
    for i in range(1, n):
        if ratings[i] > ratings[i-1]:
            left[i] = left[i-1] + 1
    
    right = [1] * n
    for i in range(n-2, -1, -1):
        if ratings[i] > ratings[i+1]:
            right[i] = right[i+1] + 1
    
    total = sum(max(left[i], right[i]) for i in range(n))
    print(total)

if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
```

### Explanation
- **Reading Input**: The input is read using standard input for efficiency, especially with large datasets.
- **Left Pass**: Initializes each patient with 1 tablet. For each patient, if their health score is higher than the previous patient, their tablet count is incremented by the previous patient's count.
- **Right Pass**: Similar to the left pass but traverses from right to left, ensuring each patient gets more tablets than their right neighbor if their health score is higher.
- **Summing Maximum Values**: For each patient, the maximum tablets required from either the left or right pass is summed to get the minimum total tablets needed.

This approach efficiently handles the constraints with a time complexity of O(N) and space complexity of O(N), making it suitable for large input sizes.
