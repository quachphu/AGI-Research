2. **Prefix Sums**: Compute prefix sums for both even and odd lists to efficiently calculate the sum of the top k elements.
3. **Maximize Deleted Sum**: For both possible starting parities (even or odd), determine the maximum sum of elements that can be deleted by iterating through possible sequence lengths and checking valid parity alternations.
4. **Calculate Result**: Subtract the maximum possible sum of deleted elements from the total sum of the array to get the minimum sum of non-deleted elements.

# Compute prefix sums for even and odd lists
prefix_even = [0]

    current_sum += num
    prefix_even.append(current_sum)

prefix_odd = [0]
current_sum = 0

    current_sum += num
    prefix_odd.append(current_sum)

    odd_needed = t // 2

        current_sum = prefix_even[even_needed] + prefix_odd[odd_needed]
        if current_sum > max_even_start:
