To solve this problem, we need to determine the minimum total tiredness two friends will experience when they meet at a common integer point on a coordinate axis. Each friend's tiredness increases incrementally with each move they make. The goal is to find the optimal meeting point that minimizes this total tiredness.

### Approach
1. **Problem Analysis**: The tiredness of each friend is determined by the sum of the first `n` natural numbers, where `n` is the number of moves they make. This sum can be calculated using the formula for the `n`-th triangular number: `n * (n + 1) / 2`.
2. **Key Insight**: The optimal meeting point will lie between the initial positions of the two friends. This is because moving outside this range would result in unnecessary additional steps, leading to higher tiredness.
3. **Algorithm**: 
   - Calculate the minimum and maximum of the two initial positions to determine the range of possible meeting points.
   - For each point within this range, compute the total tiredness for both friends if they meet at that point.
   - Track the minimum total tiredness encountered during this iteration.

### Solution Code
```python
a = int(input())
b = int(input())

min_ab = min(a, b)
max_ab = max(a, b)

min_total = float('inf')

for x in range(min_ab, max_ab + 1):
    d1 = abs(a - x)
    d2 = abs(b - x)
    total = (d1 * (d1 + 1)) // 2 + (d2 * (d2 + 1)) // 2
    if total < min_total:
        min_total = total

print(min_total)
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The initial positions of the two friends are read from standard input.
2. **Determine Range**: The possible meeting points are between the minimum and maximum of the two initial positions.
3. **Calculate Tiredness**: For each potential meeting point within the determined range, compute the tiredness for both friends using the triangular number formula.
4. **Track Minimum**: Keep track of the minimum total tiredness encountered during the iteration over all possible meeting points.
5. **Output Result**: Print the minimum total tiredness found.

This approach efficiently checks all possible meeting points within the relevant range, ensuring that the solution is both optimal and computationally feasible.
