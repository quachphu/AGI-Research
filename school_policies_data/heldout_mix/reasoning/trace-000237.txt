   - At minute 0, all black squares are painted once.
   - For each subsequent minute `i`, a black square is repainted if all four of its diagonal neighbors were repainted in the previous minute.
   - The number of times a square is repainted depends on its distance from the edges of the board, forming layers of repainting.

   - Check if the square is black (i.e., `(i + j) % 2 == 0`).
