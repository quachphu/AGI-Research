To solve this problem, we need to determine the maximum number of trees that can be cut down such that the segments occupied by the fallen trees do not overlap with each other or include the coordinates of any other trees. The trees are given in ascending order of their coordinates, and each tree can be cut to the left, right, or left standing.

### Approach
1. **Initialization**: Track the maximum count of trees cut and the furthest right position occupied by the segments of the fallen trees.
2. **Processing Each Tree**: For each tree, evaluate the possibility of cutting it to the left, right, or leaving it standing:
   - **Cutting Left**: Check if the left segment does not overlap with previous segments and does not include any previous tree's coordinate.
   - **Cutting Right**: Check if the right segment does not overlap with previous segments and does not include the next tree's coordinate (if it exists).
3. **Update State**: Choose the option (cut left, cut right, or leave) that maximizes the count of cut trees and leaves the earliest possible rightmost position for future trees to allow more cuts.
