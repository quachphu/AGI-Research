Alice's graph is a subset of a complete graph, and Bob's graph is the complement of Alice's graph. ### Approach
1. **Understand the Problem**: Each triangle in Alice's graph must have all three edges present in her graph, while each triangle in Bob's graph must have all three edges absent from Alice's graph. The goal is to find the sum of these triangles in both graphs.

2. **Mathematical Insight**:
   - The total number of triangles in a complete graph with `n` vertices is given by `C(n, 3) = n*(n-1)*(n-2)/6`.
   - The number of triangles in Alice's and Bob's graphs combined can be derived using the sum of degrees of vertices and combinatorial calculations.

3. **Key Formula**:
   - Calculate the sum of combinations of degrees of each vertex taken two at a time.
   - Use the formula: `Total Triangles = C(n, 3) - m*(n-2) + sum(d_i choose 2)`, where `m` is the number of edges in Alice's graph and `d_i` is the degree of vertex `i`.

### Solution Code
```python
n, m = map(int, input().split())
degrees = [0] * (n + 1)

for _ in range(m):
    u, v = map(int, input().split())
    degrees[u] += 1
    degrees[v] += 1

sum_d_choose_2 = sum(d * (d - 1) // 2 for d in degrees)

if n >= 3:
    total_triangles = n * (n - 1) * (n - 2) // 6
else:

result = total_triangles - m * (n - 2) + sum_d_choose_2
```
