A correct step-up occurs when both feet transition from the floor to the platform, and a correct step-down occurs when both feet transition from the platform to the floor. Each transition must occur in two consecutive steps, either in left-right or right-left order.

### Approach
1. **Track State Transitions**: We maintain the current state of each foot (left and right) as either on the floor (False) or platform (True).
2. **Check Valid Transitions**: After each movement, check if the current state transitions from the floor to the platform (step-up) or from the platform to the floor (step-down). This is done by comparing the current state with the previous two states to ensure the transition is valid.
3. **Count Valid Steps**: Increment the count each time a valid step-up or step-down transition is detected.
