A quadrilateral is convex if all its interior angles are less than 180 degrees, which can be checked by ensuring that all turns between consecutive edges are in the same direction.

### Approach
1. **Understanding Convexity**: A polygon is convex if all its interior angles are less than 180 degrees. This can be checked using the cross product of consecutive edge vectors. If all cross products have the same sign (all positive or all negative), the polygon is convex.
2. **Cross Product Calculation**: For each set of three consecutive points (A, B, C), compute the cross product of the vectors AB and BC. This cross product indicates the direction of the turn. If all cross products for the four edges of the quadrilateral are either all positive or all negative, the quadrilateral is convex.
3. **Edge Vectors**: For each edge transition (AB to BC, BC to CD, CD to DA, DA to AB), compute the cross product to check the direction of the turn.

import sys

def main():
    for line in sys.stdin:
        line = line.strip()
