To solve this problem, we need to determine the smallest value of \( k \) such that Awruk wins the election by having strictly more votes than his opponent, Elodreip. Each student gives \( a_i \) votes to Elodreip, and the remaining \( k - a_i \) votes go to Awruk. The value of \( k \) must be at least the maximum number of votes any student gives to Elodreip.

### Approach
1. **Sum Calculation**: Calculate the total number of votes Elodreip receives, which is the sum of all elements in the list \( a \).
2. **Determine Minimum \( k \)**: Compute the minimum \( k \) required for Awruk to win using the formula derived from the condition that Awruk's votes must exceed Elodreip's votes. This formula is \( \text{required} = \left\lfloor \frac{2 \times \text{sum\_a}}{n} \right\rfloor + 1 \).
3. **Check Maximum Constraint**: Ensure \( k \) is at least the maximum value in the list \( a \).

### Solution Code
```python
n = int(input())
a = list(map(int, input().split()))
sum_a = sum(a)
max_a = max(a)
required = (2 * sum_a // n) + 1
k = max(required, max_a)
print(k)
```

### Explanation
1. **Input Reading**: Read the number of students \( n \) and the list \( a \) of votes each student gives to Elodreip.
2. **Sum and Maximum Calculation**: Compute the total votes for Elodreip and the maximum votes any single student gives to Elodreip.
3. **Required \( k \) Calculation**: Calculate the smallest \( k \) that ensures Awruk's total votes exceed Elodreip's using integer arithmetic to avoid floating-point inaccuracies.
4. **Final \( k \) Determination**: The final value of \( k \) is the maximum between the computed required \( k \) and the maximum value in \( a \), ensuring all constraints are met.

This approach efficiently computes the minimal \( k \) using basic arithmetic operations, ensuring optimal performance even for the upper limits of input constraints.
