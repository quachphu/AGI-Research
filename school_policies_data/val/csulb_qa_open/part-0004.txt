Published by: Associated Students, Inc. (ASI) Associated Students, Incorporated (ASI) provides a wide variety of programs and services intended to enhance the educational experiences of its members, the students at California State University, Long Beach (CSULB). ASI is committed to the development and preservation of a vibrant student community and believes firmly that student engagement plays a vital role in that effort. Each year, ASI sets aside a portion of its budget into the ASI Student Research Fund. To ensure a fair and impartial basis for awarding grants from this fund, the ASI Policy on Student Research Fund has been developed. This policy is intended to articulate who is eligible for these awards, what types of research qualify, and how much can be awarded. It is the policy of ASI to support students from all academic disciplines by awarding grants for student research which is directly related to their program of study, is of at least one academic session in duration, and under the guidance of sponsoring faculty members. Through the Student Research Fund, students may apply for funding for the following expenses: Research-Related Supplies Research-Related Travel Research Findings Publication

Section: Eligibility
Source: https://asicsulb.org/corporate/documents/gov/2026-27%20Policy%20on%20Student%20Research%20Fund%20(FINAL).pdf
To be eligible for an award from the Student Research Fund: 2.0 Applicant (Applicants, in the case of a co-authored research project) must be regularly admitted and enrolled (matriculated), fee paying member of the ASI at the time the application for funding is submitted. Application must be submitted no later than three (3) weeks prior to commencing research activity. In the case that research necessitates international travel, the application must be submitted no later than six (6) weeks prior to commencing research activity. "Activity" is defined as the active process of collecting information and/or conducting research, typically guided by a previous planning phase. In the case of a co-authored research project, one application listing all co-authors names is to be submitted for group funding consideration. Grants cannot be awarded for research activity already in progress or which have been completed. Research must be directly related to the applicant's program of study and should represent a significant contribution to the student's educational experience. Applicant may apply for and receive one Student Research Fund award per semester. Research must be at least one academic session in duration, and under the guidance and supervision of a CSULB faculty member. To avoid the appearance of taxable compensation, research grants will be awarded on a reimbursement basis. Applicant must have paid all the expenses.
