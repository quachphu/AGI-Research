To solve this problem, we need to determine the maximum number of bricks that can be stacked such that each brick remains stable under the combined weight of all bricks above it. The stability of each brick is determined by ensuring the center of mass of all bricks above it lies within its base.

### Approach
1. **Input Parsing**: Read the number of bricks and their coordinates.
2. **Brick Properties Calculation**: For each brick, calculate its side length, the coordinates of its base, and the center of its base.
3. **Stability Check**: For each brick in the stack, check if the center of mass of all bricks above it (excluding itself) lies within its base. This check is performed for all bricks from the bottom up.
4. **Iterate and Validate**: Check each possible stack size from 1 to n to determine the maximum stable stack size. If any stack size fails the stability check, the process stops and returns the maximum valid size found.
