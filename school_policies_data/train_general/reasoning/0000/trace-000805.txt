Each candy increases a senator's loyalty by 10%, up to 100%. The proposal can be approved either through a majority vote or, if the vote fails, by killing the opposing senators.

### Approach
1. **Generate All Possible Candy Distributions**: We use a backtracking approach to generate all valid distributions of up to k candies among the senators. Each senator can receive a number of candies that does not exceed their maximum possible increase (determined by their initial loyalty) and the total number of available candies.

2. **Calculate Approval Probability for Each Distribution**: For each valid distribution of candies:
   - Compute the new loyalty for each senator.
   - Determine the probability of the proposal being approved through a majority vote.
   - If the vote fails, compute the probability of killing the opposing senators and add this to the total probability.

3. **Evaluate All Voting Outcomes**: For each distribution, evaluate all possible voting outcomes (2^n possibilities for n senators). Calculate the probability of each outcome and determine if it results in a majority approval. If not, compute the probability of successfully killing the opposing senators.
