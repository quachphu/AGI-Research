To solve this problem, we need to determine the maximum number of coconuts Sasha and Masha can buy by possibly exchanging chizhiks, and the minimum number of chizhiks they need to exchange to achieve this maximum.

### Approach
1. **Initial Calculation**: First, compute the initial number of coconuts each can buy without any exchange. This is done by dividing their respective chizhiks by the price per coconut.
2. **Maximum Possible Coconuts**: The maximum possible coconuts they can buy together is determined by the total chizhiks they have combined, divided by the price per coconut.
3. **Check for Exchange Need**: If the initial count of coconuts equals the maximum possible, no exchange is needed. Otherwise, compute the minimum exchange required.
4. **Minimum Exchange Calculation**: The minimum exchange required is derived from the remainders of their chizhiks when divided by the coconut price. The exchange needed is the smallest adjustment to make one of the remainders contribute to an additional coconut.

### Solution Code

x, y, z = map(int, input().split())

4. **Remainders and Exchange**: If an exchange is needed, the minimum number of chizhiks required to transfer is determined by the smaller value needed to adjust the remainders to reach a multiple of the coconut price, thus allowing an additional coconut purchase.
