To solve this problem, we need to determine the maximum number of fixed-orientation rectangular tiles that can be placed on a rectangular wall and calculate the remaining uncovered area after placing these tiles.

### Approach
1. **Understand the Problem Constraints**: The tiles cannot be rotated, stacked, or placed diagonally. This means the tiles must be placed in their given orientation, with their edges parallel to the wall's edges.
2. **Calculate Tiles Fit in Each Dimension**: Compute how many tiles can fit vertically by dividing the wall's height by the tile's height. Similarly, compute how many tiles can fit horizontally by dividing the wall's width by the tile's width.
3. **Compute Total Covered Area**: Multiply the number of tiles that fit vertically and horizontally to get the total number of tiles. Multiply this by the area of each tile to get the total covered area.
4. **Determine Uncovered Area**: Subtract the total covered area from the wall's total area to get the uncovered area.

### Solution Code
```python
def calculate_uncovered_area():
    import sys
    h, w = map(int, sys.stdin.readline().split())
    a, b = map(int, sys.stdin.readline().split())
    num_tiles_height = h // a
    num_tiles_width = w // b
    total_tiles = num_tiles_height * num_tiles_width
    covered_area = total_tiles * a * b
    uncovered_area = h * w - covered_area
    print(uncovered_area)

calculate_uncovered_area()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The function reads the wall's dimensions (height and width) and the tile's dimensions (height and width) from standard input.
2. **Tiles Calculation**: It calculates how many tiles fit vertically (`num_tiles_height`) and horizontally (`num_tiles_width`) using integer division.
3. **Total Covered Area**: The total number of tiles is the product of tiles fitting vertically and horizontally. Multiplying this by the area of each tile gives the total area covered by the tiles.
4. **Uncovered Area Calculation**: The uncovered area is the difference between the wall's total area and the covered area, which is then printed as the result.

This approach efficiently computes the maximum number of tiles that can be placed and the resulting uncovered area, adhering to all given constraints.
