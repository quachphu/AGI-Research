### Approach
The problem can be efficiently solved using a mathematical approach derived from the Josephus problem with step size 2. The key insight is to find the largest power of two less than or equal to the given number of people, `n`. The solution then uses this power of two to compute the optimal position using the formula:

1. Find the largest power of two, `L`, such that `L ≤ n`.
2. If `n` is exactly a power of two, the optimal position is 1.
3. Otherwise, the optimal position is calculated as `2 * (n - L) + 1`.
