### Approach

The solution involves recursively checking the left and right parts of the string at each step, considering the middle character, and combining the results to find the longest common substring.

1. **Recursive Decomposition**: For each step, decompose the problem into checking the left and right parts of the string, adjusting the ranges as needed.

3. **Mirroring Check**: Due to the palindrome nature of the string, check mirrored parts of the string for potential common substrings.
4. **Early Termination**: Use early termination to avoid unnecessary recursive calls if the current best candidate is already optimal.
