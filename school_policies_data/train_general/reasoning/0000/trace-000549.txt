### Approach
1. **Grundy Numbers Calculation**: Grundy numbers (or nimbers) are used to determine the winning positions in impartial games. For each pile size, we compute the Grundy number by considering all possible valid splits and taking the minimum excludant (mex) of the resulting values.
2. **Check Splits**: For each possible pile size, we check all valid splits into consecutive numbers and compute the Grundy number for each split.
3. **Determine Winner**: Using the precomputed Grundy numbers, we determine if Serozha can win by finding the minimal number of piles he can split into such that the resulting position is a losing position for Gena.
