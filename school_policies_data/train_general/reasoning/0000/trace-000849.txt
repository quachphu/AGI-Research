Each team earns points based on the outcomes of their matches, and the ranking is determined by their total points. Teams with the same points share the same rank, and the next team's rank is adjusted accordingly.

### Approach
1. **Input Reading**: Read the number of teams and the results of each match.
2. **Points Calculation**: For each match, update the points for the respective teams based on the match outcome (win, loss, or draw).
3. **Sorting Teams**: Sort the teams in descending order of their points. If two teams have the same points, they are sorted by their team number in ascending order.
4. **Rank Assignment**: Assign ranks to the teams considering ties. Teams with the same points receive the same rank, and subsequent ranks are adjusted to reflect the number of teams that have already been ranked.
