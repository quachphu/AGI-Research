Given the constraints, a direct approach of generating and checking each number would be inefficient, so we use a more optimized method involving prime factorization, Möbius function, and binary search.

### Approach
1. **Precompute Smallest Prime Factors (SPF):** This helps in efficiently factorizing any number up to 1e6 into its prime factors.
2. **Factorize p:** For each query, factorize p into its distinct prime factors.
3. **Generate Divisors and Möbius Values:** For each subset of the prime factors, generate the product of the primes in the subset and compute the Möbius function value.
4. **Binary Search:** Use binary search to find the smallest number greater than x such that there are at least k coprime numbers between x and this number. The count of coprimes is computed using the inclusion-exclusion principle.
