To solve this problem, we need to split four people into two teams of two each such that the difference in their combined ratings is minimized. The goal is to find the minimum possible absolute difference between the sums of the ratings of the two teams.

### Approach
1. **Understanding Team Combinations**: Given four people with ratings \(a, b, c, d\), there are three distinct ways to form two teams of two each:
   - Team 1: \(a + b\) and Team 2: \(c + d\)
   - Team 1: \(a + c\) and Team 2: \(b + d\)
   - Team 1: \(a + d\) and Team 2: \(b + c\)

2. **Calculate Differences**: For each of these combinations, compute the absolute difference between the sums of the two teams.

3. **Find Minimum Difference**: The minimum value among these computed differences is the answer.

### Solution Code
```python
a, b, c, d = map(int, input().split())

diff1 = abs((a + b) - (c + d))
diff2 = abs((a + c) - (b + d))
diff3 = abs((a + d) - (b + c))

print(min(diff1, diff2, diff3))
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The input consists of four integers representing the ratings of the four individuals.
2. **Compute Differences**: For each of the three possible team combinations, compute the absolute difference between the sums of the two teams.
3. **Determine Minimum**: The minimum of these three differences is the smallest possible difference in team strengths, which is then printed as the result.

This approach efficiently checks all possible valid team combinations and ensures that we find the optimal solution with minimal computational overhead. The complexity is constant time \(O(1)\) since we only compute three differences regardless of the input size.
