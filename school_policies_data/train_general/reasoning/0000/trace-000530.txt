To solve this problem, we need to convert a given length in centimeters to feet and inches according to specific conversion and rounding rules. The conversion involves first converting centimeters to inches and then further converting the inches to feet and remaining inches.

### Approach
1. **Conversion from Centimeters to Inches**: 
   - Each inch in Baden is equivalent to 3 centimeters. 
   - When converting, if the remaining centimeters after division by 3 is 1 or 2, we round to the nearest inch. Specifically, 1 cm rounds down to 0 inches, and 2 cm rounds up to 1 inch.
   
2. **Conversion from Inches to Feet and Inches**:
   - After converting centimeters to inches, we convert the total inches to feet. Each foot is 12 inches. The remaining inches after converting to feet are the result.

### Solution Code
```python
def convert_cm_to_feet_inches():
    n = int(input())
    total_inches = (n // 3) + (1 if (n % 3 >= 2) else 0)
    feet = total_inches // 12
    inches = total_inches % 12
    print(feet, inches)

convert_cm_to_feet_inches()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The input is read as an integer which represents the length in centimeters.
2. **Converting to Inches**: The total inches are calculated by dividing the centimeters by 3. If the remainder of this division is 2 or more, we add an additional inch to account for the rounding rule.
3. **Converting to Feet and Remaining Inches**: The total inches are then divided by 12 to get the number of feet, with the remainder giving the remaining inches.
4. **Output**: The result is printed as two integers representing feet and remaining inches.

This approach ensures that the conversion adheres to the specified rounding rules and efficiently computes the required values using integer arithmetic.
