3. **Collision Detection**: For each segment of the projectile's path, check if it intersects the origin or the virtual wall. If it hits the wall, compute the reflected velocity vector and continue the simulation.
4. **Distance Calculation**: Track the accumulated distance traveled by the projectile. If it reaches the origin within the allowed distance, output the distance; otherwise, output "impossible".
