To solve this problem, we need to determine the number of days Alice will take to reach the top of a staircase with N stairs, given that she climbs A stairs during the day and slides down B stairs at night. The key is to find an efficient solution that avoids simulating each day step-by-step, especially since N can be very large (up to 10^9).

### Approach
1. **Immediate Climb Check**: If the number of stairs Alice climbs in one day (A) is greater than or equal to the total stairs (N), she will reach the top on the first day itself.
2. **Effective Daily Progress**: For each subsequent day, Alice effectively progresses (A - B) stairs because she climbs A stairs during the day and slides down B stairs at night. This continues until she is close enough to the top such that her next climb will take her to or beyond the top.
3. **Mathematical Calculation**: Using the effective daily progress, we can compute the number of days required using a formula. The formula accounts for the remaining stairs after the initial climb and the effective daily progress.

### Solution Code
```python
A, B, N = map(int, input().split())
if N <= A:
    print(1)
else:
    step = A - B
    remainder = N - A
    days = (remainder + step - 1) // step
    print(days + 1)
```

### Explanation
1. **Immediate Climb Check**: If the total stairs (N) are less than or equal to the stairs Alice climbs in a day (A), she reaches the top on the first day. This is checked and handled immediately.
2. **Effective Daily Progress**: For each day after the first, Alice's net progress is (A - B) stairs. We need to calculate how many such days are required until she is within A stairs of the top.
3. **Formula Calculation**: The formula `(remainder + step - 1) // step` computes the number of full days required to reach the remaining stairs after the first climb. Here, `remainder` is the stairs left after the first climb (N - A), and `step` is the effective daily progress (A - B). The result is adjusted by adding 1 to account for the initial day's climb.

This approach efficiently calculates the number of days using mathematical operations, ensuring optimal performance even for large values of N.
