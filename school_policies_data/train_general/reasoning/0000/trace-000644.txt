### Approach
1. **Classify Segments**: Each segment must be either horizontal (same y-coordinate for endpoints) or vertical (same x-coordinate for endpoints). If any segment is neither, the answer is "NO".
2. **Check Validity**: Ensure there are exactly two horizontal and two vertical segments. Each segment must be non-degenerate (i.e., have a positive length).
3. **Determine Coordinates**: Collect the x-coordinates from vertical segments and y-coordinates from horizontal segments. These should form two distinct x-values (left and right edges) and two distinct y-values (bottom and top edges).
4. **Validate Edges**: Check that each horizontal segment spans the full width between the left and right edges, and each vertical segment spans the full height between the bottom and top edges.
5. **Check Area**: Ensure the calculated width and height are positive to guarantee a positive area.
