To solve this problem, we need to determine the members of the divine tribe based on the answers given by the inhabitants of the island. The challenge is to use the given constraints (truth-tellers vs liars) and the population sizes of the tribes to uniquely identify all divine members.

### Approach
1. **Union-Find Data Structure with Parity**: We use a union-find data structure to group inhabitants based on their answers. Each node (inhabitant) tracks its parent and a parity value indicating whether it is equal to its parent or not. This helps in efficiently merging sets and checking constraints.
2. **Dynamic Programming (DP) for Combinations**: After grouping inhabitants into components based on their answers, we use dynamic programming to determine the number of ways to select components such that the total number of divine members matches the given population (p1).
3. **Backtracking for Unique Solution**: If there's exactly one valid combination, we backtrack to determine which components contribute to this solution and collect the divine members accordingly.
