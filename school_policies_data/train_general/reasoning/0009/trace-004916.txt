To solve this problem, we need to determine the probability that Alice will enjoy her school trip given that she is already selected to go. Alice will enjoy the trip if at least K of her friends are also selected. The solution involves calculating this probability using combinatorial mathematics.

### Approach
1. **Understanding the Problem**: Alice's enjoyment depends on having at least K friends with her on the trip. We need to calculate this probability given that she is already part of the selected group.
2. **Combinatorial Analysis**: 
   - **Total Possible Ways**: Calculate the total number of ways to select the remaining students after Alice is chosen. This is given by the combination formula `C(S-1, N-1)`.
   - **Favorable Ways**: Calculate the number of ways to select students such that at least K friends of Alice are included. This involves summing up combinations where the number of friends selected ranges from K to the maximum possible friends available.
3. **Constraints Handling**: Ensure that the number of friends and non-friends selected does not exceed their available counts and the total students to be picked.
