### Approach
The problem can be approached using Breadth-First Search (BFS) to explore all possible states of the ball arrangement. Each state transition corresponds to a valid swap of ball #1 with one of its neighbors in a different row. The BFS ensures that we find the shortest path (minimum swaps) from the initial configuration to the correct configuration.

1. **State Representation**: Each state is represented by the current arrangement of the balls, the position of ball #1, and the number of swaps taken to reach that state.
2. **Correct Configuration**: Precompute the correct positions of all balls in the triangular setup.
3. **Neighbor Calculation**: For each position of ball #1, determine its valid neighbors in adjacent rows (both above and below the current row).
4. **BFS Traversal**: Use BFS to explore all possible states starting from the initial configuration. Track visited states to avoid redundant checks and ensure the shortest path is found.
