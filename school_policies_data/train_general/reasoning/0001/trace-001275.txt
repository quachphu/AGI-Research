### Approach
1. **Initial Distance Check**: If Alice's starting position is within her maximum jump distance from Bob's starting position, Alice wins immediately.
2. **Tree Diameter Check**: If the diameter of the tree is at most twice Alice's maximum jump distance, Alice can corner Bob eventually, ensuring her victory.
3. **Bob's Escape Check**: If Bob's maximum jump distance is more than twice Alice's, Bob can always escape Alice's attempts to catch him, leading to his victory. Otherwise, Alice can still catch Bob despite his movements.
