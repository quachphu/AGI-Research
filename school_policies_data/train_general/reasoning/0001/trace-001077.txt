To solve this problem, we need to determine how many times Takahashi can hear his favorite sound from a vending machine given his budget and a maximum limit on the number of times he wants to hear the sound.

### Approach
1. **Understand the Inputs**: The problem provides three integers: the cost per sound (A), the total money Takahashi has (B), and the maximum number of times he wants to hear the sound (C).
2. **Calculate Maximum Possible Uses Based on Money**: Using integer division, compute how many times Takahashi can afford to hear the sound with his available money. This is given by `B // A`.
3. **Determine the Actual Number of Times**: The actual number of times he will hear the sound is the minimum of the maximum possible uses based on his money and the maximum allowed times (C).

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    A, B, C = map(int, input().split())
    print(min(B // A, C))

main()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The input values are read and split into three integers: A, B, and C.
2. **Compute Maximum Uses Based on Money**: Using integer division `B // A`, we determine how many times Takahashi can afford to hear the sound with his available money.
3. **Determine the Result**: The result is the minimum value between the computed maximum uses (from step 2) and the maximum allowed times (C). This ensures Takahashi does not exceed either his budget or his desired maximum number of uses.

This approach efficiently combines straightforward arithmetic operations and a comparison to arrive at the solution in constant time, ensuring optimal performance even for the upper limits of the input constraints.
