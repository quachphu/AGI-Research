To solve this problem, we need to find the sum of all positive integers less than a given number \( N \) that are divisible by either of two given numbers \( A \) or \( B \). The solution should efficiently compute this sum using mathematical principles to avoid iterating through each number up to \( N \).

### Approach
1. **Mathematical Insight**: Use the inclusion-exclusion principle to avoid double-counting numbers divisible by both \( A \) and \( B \).
2. **Sum of Multiples**: Calculate the sum of multiples of \( A \) and \( B \) up to \( N-1 \) using arithmetic series formulas.
3. **Least Common Multiple (LCM)**: Compute the LCM of \( A \) and \( B \) to identify numbers that are multiples of both, which are subtracted from the total sum to correct for double-counting.

### Solution Code
```python
import math

n, a, b = map(int, input().split())

def compute_sum(k):
    m = (n - 1) // k
    return k * m * (m + 1) // 2

sum_a = compute_sum(a)
sum_b = compute_sum(b)

gcd_ab = math.gcd(a, b)
lcm_ab = a * b // gcd_ab
sum_lcm = compute_sum(lcm_ab)

total = sum_a + sum_b - sum_lcm
print(total)
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The input values \( N \), \( A \), and \( B \) are read from standard input.
2. **Sum of Multiples Calculation**:
   - **For \( A \)**: Compute the sum of all multiples of \( A \) less than \( N \) using the formula for the sum of an arithmetic series.
   - **For \( B \)**: Similarly, compute the sum of all multiples of \( B \) less than \( N \).
3. **LCM Calculation**: Determine the LCM of \( A \) and \( B \) using their greatest common divisor (GCD). This helps in identifying numbers that are multiples of both \( A \) and \( B \).
4. **Inclusion-Exclusion Principle**: Adjust the total sum by subtracting the sum of multiples of the LCM to correct for double-counting those numbers.

This approach efficiently computes the required sum using mathematical formulas, ensuring optimal performance even for large values of \( N \).
