3. **Excluding Sequences Without 9**: Similarly, the number of sequences that do not contain 9 is \( 9^N \).
4. **Including Overlap Correction**: Sequences that neither contain 0 nor 9 are counted twice in the previous exclusions. These sequences are \( 8^N \) (each element can be 1-8).

Using the inclusion-exclusion principle, the formula to find the valid sequences is:
\[ \text{Answer} = (10^N - 2 \times 9^N + 8^N) \mod (10^9 + 7) \]

n = int(input())
