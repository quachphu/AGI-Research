### Approach
1. **Understanding the LCM**: The numbers from 2 to 10 have a least common multiple (LCM) which is the smallest number divisible by all of them. Calculating this LCM gives us 2520.
2. **Counting Multiples**: Any multiple of 2520 within the range from 1 to n will be divisible by all numbers from 2 to 10. The count of such multiples is given by the integer division of n by 2520.

### Solution Code
```python

```
