### Approach
1. **Problem Analysis**: Directly iterating over all possible 3x3 subrectangles is infeasible due to the grid's size. Instead, we focus on how each painted cell affects the subrectangles it belongs to.
2. **Key Insight**: Each painted cell (a, b) affects a limited number of 3x3 subrectangles. We determine these subrectangles by their top-left corners (x, y) such that (a, b) lies within the subrectangle.
3. **Range Calculation**: For each painted cell (a, b), calculate the valid range of top-left corners (x, y) for the 3x3 subrectangles that include (a, b). This is done by clamping the ranges to the grid boundaries.
4. **Frequency Counting**: Use a dictionary to count how many times each subrectangle (identified by its top-left corner) is affected by painted cells.
5. **Result Calculation**: After processing all cells, compute the number of subrectangles with exactly j black cells. The count for j=0 is derived by subtracting the sum of counts for j=1 to 9 from the total number of subrectangles.

from collections import defaultdict

        x_start = max(1, a - 2)

            continue
