To solve this problem, we need to determine the year Igor entered university based on the groups he joined. Each group corresponds to a specific entrance year, and Igor's groups form a contiguous range around his entrance year. The key insight is that Igor's entrance year is the median of the sorted list of group years.

### Approach
1. **Input Reading**: Read the number of groups and the list of years.
2. **Sorting**: Sort the list of years to identify the contiguous range.
3. **Median Calculation**: Since the list is sorted and the number of groups (n) is odd, the median of the sorted list will be Igor's entrance year.

### Solution Code
```python
n = int(input())
years = list(map(int, input().split()))
years.sort()
print(years[n // 2])
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The first input line gives the number of groups, and the second line provides the list of years corresponding to these groups.
2. **Sorting the List**: By sorting the list of years, we ensure that the years are in ascending order, which helps in identifying the contiguous range around Igor's entrance year.
3. **Finding the Median**: The median of an odd-length list is the middle element when the list is sorted. This median value represents Igor's entrance year because the groups he joined form a contiguous range centered around his entrance year.

This approach efficiently determines the entrance year by leveraging the properties of sorted lists and median calculation, ensuring an optimal solution with a time complexity of O(n log n) due to sorting.
