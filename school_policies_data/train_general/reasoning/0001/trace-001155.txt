The game involves expanding a substring starting and ending at position `k` such that each new substring is lexicographically smaller than the previous one. ### Approach
The key insight is that Ann can make a move if there exists any character to the left of position `k` (i.e., in the substring `s[0..k-1]`) that is smaller than `s[k]`. If such a character exists, Ann can always choose the leftmost occurrence of the smallest character to ensure she wins. Otherwise, Mike wins because Ann cannot make a valid move.

To efficiently determine this for each position `k`, we maintain a running minimum of the characters seen so far. This allows us to check in constant time if there is any character smaller than `s[k]` in the prefix `s[0..k-1]`.
