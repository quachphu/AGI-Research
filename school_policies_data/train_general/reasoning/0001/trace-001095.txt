The cashback is calculated as the floor of 10% of the amount spent. The goal is to find the optimal strategy to maximize the total amount spent.

### Approach
The key insight is to recognize that the total amount Mishka can spend includes both the initial burles and the cumulative cashback received from each purchase. The optimal strategy involves spending in such a way that maximizes the cashback received, which can be derived using a mathematical formula.

The formula to compute the maximum total amount spent is derived as follows:
1. Each time Mishka spends an amount `x`, he receives a cashback of `floor(x/10)`.
2. The total amount spent is the initial amount `s` plus the sum of all cashbacks received.
3. The sum of cashbacks can be efficiently computed using integer division, leading to the formula `s + (s - 1) // 9`.

### Solution Code
```python

    s = int(input())
    print(s + (s - 1) // 9)
```
