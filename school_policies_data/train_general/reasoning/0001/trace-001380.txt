3. Every digit in color 1 is less than or equal to every digit in color 2.

We use a dynamic programming approach to track possible states while processing each digit. Each state is characterized by:
- `max_color1`: The maximum digit in color 1 so far.
- `last_color2`: The last digit in color 2 to ensure non-decreasing order.
- `min_color2`: The minimum digit in color 2 to ensure all color 1 digits are <= color 2 digits.

                path.append(color)
