1. **Identify Tree Centers**: The tree's center(s) are potential candidates for the root. This is because the center of a tree often provides a balanced structure, which might help in satisfying the given condition.

                leaves.append(u)

                    degree[v] -= 1

                        leaves.append(v)
        return list(leaves)

            level_size = len(q)
            current_degrees = []

                first = current_degrees[0]
                for d in current_degrees[1:]:
