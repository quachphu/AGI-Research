To solve this problem, we need to calculate the area of a right-angled triangle given the lengths of its three sides. The right angle is at vertex B, so the sides AB and BC are the legs, and CA is the hypotenuse. The area of a right-angled triangle is given by half the product of the lengths of the two legs.

### Approach
1. **Understand the Input**: The input consists of three integers representing the lengths of the sides AB, BC, and CA in that order.
2. **Identify the Legs and Hypotenuse**: Since the right angle is at vertex B, the sides AB and BC are the legs, and CA is the hypotenuse.
3. **Calculate the Area**: The area of a right-angled triangle is calculated as half the product of the lengths of the two legs. This can be directly computed using the formula \((\text{AB} \times \text{BC}) / 2\).

### Solution Code
```python
def calculate_area():
    a, b, c = map(int, input().split())
    print((a * b) // 2)

calculate_area()
```

### Explanation
- **Reading Input**: The input is read as a single line and split into three integers corresponding to the lengths of AB, BC, and CA.
- **Compute the Area**: Using the formula for the area of a right-angled triangle, we multiply the lengths of the two legs (AB and BC) and then divide by 2. Integer division is used to ensure the result is an integer, as guaranteed by the problem constraints.

This approach efficiently computes the area in constant time, \(O(1)\), since it involves only basic arithmetic operations. The solution is optimal and straightforward given the problem constraints.
