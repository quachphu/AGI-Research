def main():
    import sys
    input = sys.stdin.read().split()
    ptr = 0
    T = int(input[ptr])

    for _ in range(T):

        ptr += N
        total = sum(A)

3. **Sum Calculation**: The sum of the required candies is computed.
4. **Decision Making**: We check if the sum of required candies is less than or equal to the available candies (C) and print "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
