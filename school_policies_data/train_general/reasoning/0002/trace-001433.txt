To solve this problem, we need to determine if one card can beat another card in the game of Durak, considering the trump suit and the ranks of the cards. The solution involves checking specific conditions based on the game rules.

### Approach
1. **Understand the Trump Suit Rule**: A card can beat another card if it is of the trump suit and the other card is not, regardless of their ranks.
2. **Same Suit Rule**: If both cards are of the same suit, the card with the higher rank wins.
3. **Rank Order**: The ranks are ordered from lowest to highest as follows: "6", "7", "8", "9", "T", "J", "Q", "K", "A".

The approach involves:
1. Reading the trump suit and the two cards from the input.
2. Checking if the first card is a trump and the second is not, which immediately determines a win.
3. If the suits are the same, compare their ranks based on the predefined order.
4. If none of the above conditions are met, the first card cannot beat the second.

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    trump = input().strip()
    card1, card2 = input().split()
    
    rank_order = ['6', '7', '8', '9', 'T', 'J', 'Q', 'K', 'A']
    
    c1_rank = card1[0]
    c1_suit = card1[1]
    c2_rank = card2[0]
    c2_suit = card2[1]
    
    if c1_suit == trump and c2_suit != trump:
        print("YES")
    elif c1_suit == c2_suit:
        if rank_order.index(c1_rank) > rank_order.index(c2_rank):
            print("YES")
        else:
            print("NO")
    else:
        print("NO")

main()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The trump suit and the two cards are read from standard input.
2. **Extracting Suits and Ranks**: Each card is split into its rank and suit components.
3. **Trump Suit Check**: If the first card is a trump and the second is not, the result is "YES".
4. **Same Suit Check**: If both cards have the same suit, their ranks are compared using their positions in the predefined rank order list. The card with the higher rank wins.
5. **Default Case**: If none of the conditions are met, the result is "NO".

This approach efficiently checks the necessary conditions to determine if the first card can beat the second according to the rules of Durak.
