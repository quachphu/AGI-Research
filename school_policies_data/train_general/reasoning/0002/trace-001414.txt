To solve this problem, we need to compute the sum of health points restored from eating two takoyakis over all possible pairs. Each pair's contribution to the health points is the product of their deliciousness values. 

### Approach
The key insight here is to use a mathematical formula to efficiently compute the sum of products of all pairs. The formula leverages the algebraic identity that the sum of products of all pairs can be derived from the square of the sum of all elements minus the sum of the squares of each element, divided by 2. This approach reduces the time complexity to O(N), making it very efficient even for the upper constraint of N=50.

### Solution Code
```python
n = int(input())
d = list(map(int, input().split()))
total_sum = sum(d)
sum_of_squares = sum(x * x for x in d)
result = (total_sum * total_sum - sum_of_squares) // 2
print(result)
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The input consists of an integer N followed by N integers representing the deliciousness values of the takoyakis.
2. **Sum Calculation**: Compute the sum of all deliciousness values.
3. **Sum of Squares Calculation**: Compute the sum of the squares of each deliciousness value.
4. **Applying the Formula**: Using the formula \((\text{sum of elements})^2 - (\text{sum of squares of elements}) / 2\), we efficiently compute the desired sum of products of all pairs. This formula works because the square of the sum of elements includes each product twice (once for each permutation of the pair), so subtracting the sum of squares and dividing by 2 gives the correct result.

This method ensures that we avoid the need for nested loops, thus optimizing the solution to run in linear time.
