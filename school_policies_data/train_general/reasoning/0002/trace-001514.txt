The center of this circle (circumcenter) is found as the intersection of the perpendicular bisectors of the sides of the triangle, and the radius (circumradius) is the distance from the circumcenter to any of the triangle's vertices.

### Approach
1. **Input Reading**: Read the number of datasets and the coordinates of the triangle vertices for each dataset.
2. **Perpendicular Bisectors**: For two sides of the triangle, compute the equations of their perpendicular bisectors. These equations are derived from the midpoints of the sides and the slopes of the perpendicular lines.
3. **Solving Linear Equations**: Convert the equations of the perpendicular bisectors into a system of linear equations and solve them to find the circumcenter coordinates.
4. **Circumradius Calculation**: Compute the radius as the Euclidean distance from the circumcenter to any of the triangle's vertices.
5. **Output Formatting**: Print the results rounded to three decimal places.

def main():
    import sys
    input = sys.stdin.read().split()
    idx = 0

    idx += 1
    for _ in range(n):
        x1 = float(input[idx])
        y1 = float(input[idx+1])
        x2 = float(input[idx+2])
        y2 = float(input[idx+3])
        x3 = float(input[idx+4])
        y3 = float(input[idx+5])
        idx +=6
        
        a1 = 2 * (x2 - x1)
        b1 = 2 * (y2 - y1)

        py = (a1 * c2 - a2 * c1) / D

main()
