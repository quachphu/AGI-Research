2. **Reduced Problem**: For numbers that are multiples of the GCD, we reduce the problem by dividing a, b, and n by their GCD. This transforms the problem into checking representability with coprime coefficients.
3. **Frobenius Number**: For coprime coefficients, the largest number that cannot be represented (Frobenius number) is given by the formula `a*b - a - b`. If the reduced n is larger than this Frobenius number, all numbers beyond this can be represented, and we use a known formula to count non-representable numbers up to this point.
4. **Dynamic Programming**: For smaller values of the reduced n, we use dynamic programming to count non-representable numbers efficiently.
