Allen moves between entrances in a cyclic manner, and each entrance processes one person per minute. The key is to efficiently compute the earliest time Allen can be processed at each entrance, considering his movement strategy.

### Approach
1. **Understanding Allen's Movement**: Allen starts at the first entrance and moves to the next entrance each minute if he is not processed. This creates a cyclic pattern of movement through the entrances.
2. **Processing Time Calculation**: For each entrance, we need to compute the earliest time Allen can be processed. This time is determined by the number of people already in the queue and Allen's cyclic movement.
3. **Mathematical Formulation**: For each entrance `i`, compute the minimum time `T_i` such that Allen arrives at entrance `i` when the queue is empty (or he is the first in line). This involves solving a congruence problem to find the earliest time `T_i` that meets the required conditions.

3. **Compute Remainder and Target**: Calculate the remainder of the base time modulo `n` and the target remainder based on the entrance index `i`.
4. **Adjust for Cyclic Movement**: Determine the minimal adjustment (delta) needed to align the base time with the cyclic movement pattern, ensuring Allen arrives when the queue is processed.
