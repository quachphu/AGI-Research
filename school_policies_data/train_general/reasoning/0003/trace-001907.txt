To solve this problem, we need to determine the minimum amount of money you need to borrow from your friend Alice to purchase a book, given the amount of money each of you has and the price of the book. If even after borrowing all of Alice's money you still can't afford the book, the result should be "NA".

### Approach
1. **Check Total Funds**: First, we check if the combined amount of money you and Alice have is at least the price of the book. If not, it's impossible to buy the book, so we output "NA".
2. **Calculate Required Borrow**: If the combined funds are sufficient, we calculate the exact amount needed from Alice. This is determined by the difference between the book's price and your current funds, ensuring it doesn't go below zero (in case you already have enough money).

### Solution Code
```python
m, f, b = map(int, input().split())
total = m + f
if total < b:
    print("NA")
else:
    print(max(0, b - m))
```

### Explanation
1. **Input Reading**: The input consists of three integers representing your money, Alice's money, and the book's price.
2. **Total Funds Check**: By summing your money and Alice's money, we determine if purchasing the book is possible. If the sum is less than the book's price, we output "NA".
3. **Borrow Calculation**: If purchasing the book is possible, we calculate how much more money is needed beyond your current funds. If your funds alone are sufficient, no money needs to be borrowed (hence `max(0, b - m)` ensures non-negative values).

This approach efficiently determines the minimum borrowing required or if it's impossible to buy the book, ensuring optimal performance with constant time complexity.
