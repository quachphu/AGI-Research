To solve this problem, we need to determine whether Masha should use the stairs or the elevator to reach Egor's floor based on the time each option takes. The solution involves calculating the time for both methods and comparing them.

### Approach
1. **Understand the Inputs**: The inputs include the floors Masha is on, the floor she wants to reach, the elevator's current floor, and the time parameters for stairs and elevator movements.
2. **Calculate Stairs Time**: The time taken using the stairs is determined by the number of floors Masha needs to traverse multiplied by the time per floor.
3. **Calculate Elevator Time**: The elevator time includes:
   - Moving from its current floor to Masha's floor.
   - Opening and closing doors at Masha's floor.
   - Moving from Masha's floor to Egor's floor.
   - Opening doors at Egor's floor.
4. **Comparison**: Compare the times for both methods and decide based on which is faster or if they are equal.

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    x, y, z, t1, t2, t3 = map(int, input().split())
    stairs_time = abs(x - y) * t1
    elevator_move = (abs(z - x) + abs(x - y)) * t2
    elevator_time = elevator_move + 3 * t3
    print("YES" if elevator_time <= stairs_time else "NO")

main()
```

### Explanation
1. **Stairs Time Calculation**: The time taken using the stairs is computed by the absolute difference between Masha's and Egor's floors multiplied by the time per floor for stairs.
2. **Elevator Time Calculation**: This includes three components:
   - The time for the elevator to move from its current floor to Masha's floor.
   - The time for the elevator to move from Masha's floor to Egor's floor.
   - Three door operations (opening at Masha's floor, closing after she enters, and opening at Egor's floor).
3. **Comparison**: If the elevator time (including movements and door operations) is less than or equal to the stairs time, Masha should take the elevator; otherwise, she should use the stairs.

This approach ensures that all factors affecting the time for both methods are considered, providing an efficient and accurate solution.
