### Approach
1. **Understanding the Problem**: Each person joins the queue at the end and can swap with the person directly in front of them if their task importance is higher. They can continue swapping until they encounter someone with higher importance or exhaust their allowed swaps.
2. **Simulation with List Operations**: For each new person, determine how many consecutive people from the end of the current queue they can swap with, based on their task importance and allowed swaps. Insert the new person at the correct position in the queue.
3. **Efficient Insertion**: Use list operations to insert each person at the correct position by counting the valid swaps they can make and adjusting their position accordingly.

### Solution Code
```python
n = int(input())
people = []
for i in range(n):
    a, c = map(int, input().split())
    people.append((a, c, i + 1))

queue = []
for a, c, idx in people:
    steps = 0
    m = len(queue)
    # Traverse backwards up to min(c, m) steps
    while steps < c and (m - steps - 1) >= 0 and queue[m - steps - 1][0] < a:
        steps += 1
    pos = m - steps
    queue.insert(pos, (a, idx))

print(' '.join(map(str, [idx for (a, idx) in queue])))
```
