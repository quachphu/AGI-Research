To solve this problem, we need to determine the maximum number of tasty cookies Takahashi can eat without dying. The challenge involves managing antidote cookies to cure stomachaches caused by poison cookies.

### Approach
1. **Understanding Cookie Types**:
   - **A**: Untasty cookies with antidotes.
   - **B**: Tasty cookies with antidotes.
   - **C**: Tasty cookies with poison.

2. **Rules**:
   - Eating a poison cookie (C) gives a stomachache, and eating another poison cookie while having a stomachache results in death.
   - Antidote cookies (A or B) cure stomachaches.

3. **Strategy**:
   - All B cookies are eaten first since they are tasty and safe.
   - The maximum number of C cookies that can be eaten is determined by the available antidotes (A + B). Each C (except the last one) must be followed by an antidote.

4. **Formula**:
   - The maximum number of C cookies is `min(C, (A + B) + 1)`, where `(A + B)` is the total antidotes available and adding 1 allows one additional C if there are enough antidotes.
   - Total tasty cookies = B (all B cookies) + maximum possible C cookies.

### Solution Code
```python
A, B, C = map(int, input().split())
max_c = min(C, (A + B) + 1)
print(B + max_c)
```

### Explanation
- **Reading Input**: The input values for A, B, and C are read.
- **Calculate Maximum C**: The maximum number of C cookies is determined by the available antidotes (A + B), allowing one additional C if possible.
- **Total Tasty Cookies**: Sum of all B cookies and the calculated maximum C cookies.

This approach ensures that Takahashi eats the maximum number of tasty cookies while safely managing his stomachaches using available antidotes.
