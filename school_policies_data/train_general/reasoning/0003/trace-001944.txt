To solve this problem, we need to connect points on a number line such that all points are connected, and the remaining points stay connected even if red or blue points are removed. The goal is to minimize the total cost of the connections.

### Approach
The solution involves two main cases based on the presence of green points:

1. **When Green Points are Present**:
   - **MST for Green Points**: Compute the Minimum Spanning Tree (MST) for all green points. This connects all green points optimally.
   - **Connect Red and Blue Points to Green**: For each red and blue point, find the nearest green point (either to the left or right) and sum these minimum distances. This ensures that red and blue points are connected to the green backbone.

2. **When Green Points are Absent**:
   - **MST for Red and Blue Points**: Compute MSTs for red and blue points separately.
   - **Minimal Edge Between Red and Blue**: Find the minimal edge connecting any red point to any blue point to ensure the entire graph is connected.
