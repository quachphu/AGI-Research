The approach involves considering different strategies for creating bouquets and selecting the one that yields the maximum number. The key strategies are:
1. **Individual Bouquets**: Making as many single-color bouquets as possible without using any mixing bouquets.
2. **Maximum Mixing Bouquets**: Using the maximum possible number of mixing bouquets and then making single-color bouquets from the remaining flowers.
3. **Near-Maximum Mixing Bouquets**: Considering scenarios where we make slightly fewer mixing bouquets (one or two less than the maximum) to see if the remaining flowers can form more single-color bouquets.

By evaluating these strategies, we can determine the optimal solution.
