3. **Divisors of \( N \)**: Check if after dividing \( N \) by the highest power of \( K \) (where \( K \) is a divisor of \( N \) but not a divisor of \( N-1 \)), the resulting quotient modulo \( K \) is 1. This ensures that after the division steps, further subtractions will reduce \( N \) to 1.
