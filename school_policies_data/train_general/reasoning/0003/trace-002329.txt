The challenge is to efficiently track the effects of these operations, especially considering the large constraints.

### Approach
1. **Track the Original Position**: 
   - Process all reverse operations in reverse order to determine the original position of the element that ends up at position K after all operations. This helps us identify which element from the initial arithmetic progression ends up at position K.

2. **Calculate Initial Value**: 
   - Compute the initial value of the identified element using the arithmetic progression formula.

3. **Apply Operations**: 
   - Process each operation in the original order to apply increments and halving operations to the element's value, considering its position changes due to reverse operations.
