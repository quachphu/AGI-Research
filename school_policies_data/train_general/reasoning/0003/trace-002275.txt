### Approach
1. **Dynamic Programming (DP) Setup**: Use two arrays, `dark` and `white`, where `dark[t]` represents the number of ways to form a pole ending with a dark disk with total thickness `t`, and `white[t]` represents the same for a white disk.
2. **Initialization**: Start with the initial conditions where a single dark disk (either thin or thick) is valid if its thickness does not exceed \( l \).
3. **Transitions**:
   - From a dark disk at thickness `t`, the next disk must be white, adding 1 cm to the thickness.
   - From a white disk at thickness `t`, the next disk must be dark, which can either be thin (1 cm) or thick (k cm), provided the new thickness does not exceed \( l \).
4. **Iterative Processing**: Process each possible thickness from 1 to \( l \), updating the `dark` and `white` arrays based on valid transitions.
5. **Result Calculation**: Sum all valid configurations ending with a dark disk with total thickness from 1 to \( l \).
