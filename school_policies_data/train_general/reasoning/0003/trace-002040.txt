To solve this problem, we need to determine the total number of digits Vanya will write when labeling books from 1 to n. The approach involves breaking down the numbers into ranges based on their digit lengths and calculating the contribution of each range to the total count of digits.

### Approach
1. **Identify Digit Ranges**: Numbers from 1 to n can be categorized based on their digit lengths. For example, numbers 1-9 have 1 digit, 10-99 have 2 digits, 100-999 have 3 digits, and so on.
2. **Calculate Contributions**: For each digit length `d`, determine the range of numbers (from `10^(d-1)` to `10^d - 1`). If the upper bound of this range exceeds `n`, adjust it to `n`. The count of numbers in this adjusted range is multiplied by `d` (the number of digits) and added to the total.
3. **Iterate Until Completion**: Continue this process for each subsequent digit length until the lower bound of the range exceeds `n`.

### Solution Code
```python
n = int(input())
total = 0
d = 1
while True:
    lower = 10 ** (d - 1)
    upper = (10 ** d) - 1
    current_upper = min(upper, n)
    if lower > n:
        break
    count = current_upper - lower + 1
    total += count * d
    d += 1
print(total)
```

### Explanation
1. **Initialization**: Start with the smallest digit length `d = 1`.
2. **Range Calculation**: For each digit length `d`, compute the lower and upper bounds of the range (`10^(d-1)` to `10^d - 1`).
3. **Adjust Upper Bound**: If the computed upper bound exceeds `n`, adjust it to `n`.
4. **Count Numbers**: Calculate the number of integers in the adjusted range and multiply by `d` to get the total digits contributed by this range.
5. **Update Total**: Add the contribution of the current range to the total count of digits.
6. **Termination Check**: If the lower bound of the current range exceeds `n`, exit the loop.

This approach efficiently iterates through each possible digit length, ensuring that each range's contribution is accurately calculated and summed, leading to an optimal solution with a time complexity of O(log n) due to the logarithmic nature of digit length increases.
