To solve this problem, we need to determine the minimum total fare when choosing between ordinary and unlimited tickets for both trains and buses. The goal is to find the optimal combination of ticket choices to minimize the total cost.

### Approach
1. **Problem Analysis**: The problem requires us to compare the costs of ordinary and unlimited tickets for both trains and buses. For each mode of transportation (train and bus), we need to choose the cheaper option between the ordinary ticket and the unlimited ticket.
2. **Intuition**: For each mode of transportation, the minimum cost will be the lesser of the two ticket options. By calculating the minimum cost for both the train and bus separately and then summing these minima, we obtain the minimum total fare.
3. **Algorithm**:
   - Read the input values for the train and bus ticket costs.
   - Compute the minimum cost for the train tickets by comparing the ordinary and unlimited ticket prices.
   - Compute the minimum cost for the bus tickets similarly.
   - Sum the minimum costs obtained for both the train and bus to get the total minimum fare.

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    A = int(input())
    B = int(input())
    C = int(input())
    D = int(input())
    total = min(A, B) + min(C, D)
    print(total)

main()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The input consists of four integers, each on a separate line, representing the costs of ordinary and unlimited tickets for trains and buses respectively.
2. **Calculating Minimum Costs**: For both the train and bus, we determine the minimum cost by comparing the ordinary ticket price with the unlimited ticket price using the `min` function.
3. **Summing Costs**: The total minimum fare is obtained by summing the minimum costs for the train and bus tickets.
4. **Output**: The result is printed as the final output.

This approach ensures that we efficiently determine the optimal ticket choices for both modes of transportation, leading to the minimum possible total fare. The solution runs in constant time, O(1), as it involves a fixed number of operations regardless of the input size.
