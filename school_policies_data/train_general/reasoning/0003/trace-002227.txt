To solve this problem, we need to compress a given string by rearranging its characters and then applying specific replacement operations. The goal is to find the minimum possible length of the string after compression.

### Approach
1. **Rearrangement**: The characters in the string can be rearranged in any order. The optimal arrangement is to group characters into contiguous sequences of letters (from the alphabet) and digits (from 0-9) to maximize the use of hyphenation.
2. **Hyphenation Rules**:
   - Replace contiguous substrings of the alphabet (e.g., "abcde") with "a-e".
   - Replace contiguous substrings of digits (e.g., "01234") with "0-4".
   - Hyphenation is only beneficial for sequences of length 3 or more, as shorter sequences would not reduce the length.

3. **Optimal Grouping**:
   - For both letters and digits, count the occurrences of each unique character.
   - Identify the longest contiguous sequences of unique characters and determine the minimum frequency of characters in these sequences.
   - Use these sequences to calculate the contribution to the compressed length, considering the hyphenation rules.
