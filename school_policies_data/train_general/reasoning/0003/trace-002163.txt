To solve this problem, we need to determine the number of hours remaining until New Year's Day (January 1st, 00:00) starting from a given time on December 30th. The given time is provided in 24-hour notation as an integer input M.

### Approach
1. **Understand the Time Frame**: New Year's Day starts at 00:00 on January 1st. We need to calculate the hours from the given time on December 30th to this moment.
2. **Break Down the Days**:
   - From the given hour M on December 30th to midnight (24:00) of the same day, there are (24 - M) hours.
   - From midnight of December 31st (00:00) to midnight of January 1st (00:00), there are 24 hours.
3. **Sum the Hours**: The total hours remaining is the sum of the hours from December 30th to December 31st and the full day of December 31st, which simplifies to (24 - M) + 24 = 48 - M.

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    m = int(input())
    print(48 - m)

main()
```

### Explanation
- **Reading Input**: The input is read as an integer M, representing the current hour on December 30th.
- **Calculate Hours Remaining**: Using the formula 48 - M, we compute the total hours from the given time to New Year's Day. This formula accounts for the remaining hours on December 30th and the full 24 hours on December 31st.
- **Output the Result**: The result is printed directly, providing the number of hours left until New Year's Day.

This approach efficiently computes the result in constant time O(1) with minimal computational overhead, making it highly efficient for the given constraints.
