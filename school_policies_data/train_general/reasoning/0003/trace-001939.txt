To solve this problem, we need to determine the minimum time required for Genos to bring all passengers to the ground floor (floor 0) using an elevator that starts at the top floor (floor s) and can only move downwards. Each passenger arrives at a specific floor at a specific time, and the elevator can only pick up passengers who have already arrived.

### Approach
The key insight to solve this problem efficiently is to recognize that the elevator must wait for any passenger who arrives after the elevator could have reached their floor on its initial downward journey from the top floor. For each passenger, the time required to get them to the ground floor is the maximum of two values:
1. The time it takes for the elevator to reach their floor from the top floor and then descend to the ground floor (which is exactly `s` seconds since moving down from the top to their floor and then to the ground covers `s` floors in total).
2. The sum of the passenger's arrival time and the time it takes to descend from their floor to the ground floor (which is `ti + fi` where `ti` is the arrival time and `fi` is the floor number).

The overall solution is the maximum of these values across all passengers, as this represents the longest time any single passenger will take to reach the ground floor.

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    import sys
    input = sys.stdin.read().split()
    idx = 0
    n = int(input[idx])
    idx += 1
    s = int(input[idx])
    idx += 1
    max_time = 0
    for _ in range(n):
        fi = int(input[idx])
        idx += 1
        ti = int(input[idx])
        idx += 1
        current = max(s, ti + fi)
        if current > max_time:
            max_time = current
    print(max_time)

main()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input:** The input is read in one go and split into a list of strings for easier processing.
2. **Initialization:** The number of passengers `n` and the top floor `s` are read first.
3. **Processing Each Passenger:** For each passenger, their floor `fi` and arrival time `ti` are read. The maximum time required to get this passenger to the ground floor is calculated as `max(s, ti + fi)`.
4. **Determining the Result:** The maximum value from all passengers' calculated times is determined and printed as the result.

This approach ensures that we efficiently compute the minimum time required by considering the worst-case scenario for each passenger, thus guaranteeing an optimal solution.
