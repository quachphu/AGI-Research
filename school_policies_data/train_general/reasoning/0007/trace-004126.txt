The taxi can refuel at LPG stations and the destination city, and we need to ensure the driver does not run out of fuel during the journey.

### Approach
1. **Graph Representation**: Represent the cities and roads using an adjacency list to efficiently manage the connections and distances between cities.
2. **Priority Queue for Dijkstra's Algorithm**: Use a modified Dijkstra's algorithm to find the shortest path, where each state in the priority queue includes the current city, remaining fuel, and total distance traveled.
3. **Fuel Management**: Track the remaining fuel at each city and consider both possibilities when arriving at an LPG station: refueling to full capacity or continuing without refueling. The destination city is treated as an LPG station where refueling is allowed.
4. **State Tracking**: Maintain a dictionary to keep track of the minimum distance required to reach each city with a specific amount of remaining fuel to avoid redundant processing.
