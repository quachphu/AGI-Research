Our goal is to choose k and m such that the maximum number of weights can be uniquely determined based on the friend's response.

### Approach
1. **Problem Analysis**: The key insight is to find a subset (k, m) such that the subset of weights returned by the friend is unique. If the subset is unique, we can determine the masses of the weights in the subset. Additionally, we can sometimes determine the masses of the remaining weights if their combination is also unique.

2. **Dynamic Programming for Subset Sum**: We use dynamic programming to check if a given sum can be formed with a specific number of elements. This helps in determining if there are multiple ways to form the sum with different combinations of weights.

3. **Two Cases**:
   - **Case 1**: The subset (k, m) consists of homogeneous elements (all the same weight). If the remaining weights after removing this subset are also homogeneous, we can determine all weights.
   - **Case 2**: The remaining weights after removing the subset (k, m) are homogeneous. If the subset is unique, we can determine the remaining weights.
