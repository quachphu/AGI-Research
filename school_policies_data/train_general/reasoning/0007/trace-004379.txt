   - The difference between the number of odd elements and `k` must be even (since each subsegment needs an odd number of odd elements, and the sum of `k` odd numbers must have the same parity as the total number of odd elements).
3. **Determine Split Points**: If the conditions are met, the split points are determined by taking the positions of the first `k-1` odd elements (converted to 1-based indices) and appending the length of the array as the last split point.

    for _ in range(q):
        n, k = map(int, sys.stdin.readline().split())

            print("YES")
