To solve this problem, we need to calculate the internal angle between the hour and minute hands of a wall clock given a time in HH:MM format. The solution involves understanding the movement of both hands and computing their positions to determine the smallest angle between them.

### Approach
1. **Understanding Hand Movements**:
   - The minute hand moves 360 degrees in 60 minutes, translating to 6 degrees per minute.
   - The hour hand moves 360 degrees in 12 hours, translating to 30 degrees per hour. Additionally, it moves 0.5 degrees per minute as it progresses each hour.

2. **Position Calculation**:
   - **Hour Hand**: Calculate the position using the formula `(H % 12) * 30 + 0.5 * M`, where `H` is the hour and `M` is the minutes.
   - **Minute Hand**: Calculate the position using `6 * M`.

3. **Angle Calculation**:
   - Compute the absolute difference between the positions of the hour and minute hands.
   - Determine the smallest angle by considering both the calculated difference and its complement to 360 degrees (since the maximum angle on a clock is 180 degrees).

4. **Precision Handling**:
   - Ensure the result is formatted to six decimal places using Python's string formatting.

### Solution Code
```python
import sys

def calculate_angle():
    time = sys.stdin.readline().strip()
    h_str, m_str = time.split(':')
    h = int(h_str)
    m = int(m_str)
    hour_angle = (h % 12) * 30 + 0.5 * m
    minute_angle = 6 * m
    difference = abs(hour_angle - minute_angle)
    angle = min(difference, 360 - difference)
    print("{0:.6f}".format(angle))

calculate_angle()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The input time is read from standard input and split into hours (`h`) and minutes (`m`).
2. **Hour Hand Position**: Calculated by converting the hour component to a base-12 modulus and adjusting for the minutes passed.
3. **Minute Hand Position**: Directly derived from the minutes.
4. **Angle Difference**: The absolute difference between the two positions is computed, and the smaller angle is determined by considering both the difference and its complement to 360 degrees.
5. **Output Formatting**: The result is formatted to ensure six decimal places for precision.

This approach efficiently computes the required angle using straightforward mathematical operations, ensuring accuracy and adherence to the problem constraints.
