To solve this problem, we need to simulate the behavior of a water clock consisting of interconnected aquariums. The goal is to determine the water level in specific aquariums at specified times after continuously pouring water into a starting aquarium.

### Approach
1. **Input Handling**: Read the grid dimensions, starting position, flow rate, pedestal heights, and queries.
2. **Simulation Initialization**: Initialize the water volumes for each aquarium and process each query in chronological order.
3. **Continuous Water Pouring**: For each time interval between consecutive queries, add water to the starting aquarium and handle any overflow.
4. **Overflow Handling**: When an aquarium overflows, distribute the excess water to adjacent aquariums with lower or equal pedestal heights. This is done using a queue to process cascading overflows efficiently.
5. **Query Processing**: After handling the overflow for each time interval, compute the water level for the specified aquariums at the query time.
