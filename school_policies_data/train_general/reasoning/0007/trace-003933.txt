To solve this problem, we need to determine if a given grid contains exactly one valid "+" shape. A valid "+" shape has a single center cell with non-empty cells extending in all four directions (left, right, up, and down) and no other non-empty cells outside these rays.

### Approach
1. **Identify Potential Centers**: A valid center must be a non-edge cell (i.e., not in the first or last row/column) and must have non-empty cells in all four adjacent directions (left, right, up, and down).
2. **Check Ray Validity**: For each potential center, check if the rays extending in all four directions consist of consecutive non-empty cells. Rays must start from the center and extend until a non-empty cell is encountered.
3. **Verify All Other Cells**: Ensure that all other cells outside the identified "+" shape are empty.
