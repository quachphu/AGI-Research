To solve this problem, we need to determine the minimum number of moves required for the snake's head to reach the apple on a grid, while avoiding walls and its own body. The snake's body segments follow the head in a contiguous chain, and each move must be valid to avoid collisions.

### Approach
1. **Model the Snake's State**: Represent the snake's state using the positions of all its segments. Each state is a tuple of coordinates for each segment, ordered from head to tail.
2. **Breadth-First Search (BFS)**: Use BFS to explore all possible valid moves from the initial position. BFS is suitable here as it efficiently finds the shortest path in an unweighted grid.
3. **Validity Checks**: For each move, check if the new head position is within bounds, not a wall, and does not collide with any other segment of the snake's body after the move.

            new_y = head[1] + dy
