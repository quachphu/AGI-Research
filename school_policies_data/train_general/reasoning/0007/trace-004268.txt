To solve this problem, we need to identify a triangle formed by three given points in space that is similar to a given original triangle. The similarity is determined by the ratios of the sides of the triangles, allowing a small error margin. 

### Approach
1. **Input Reading**: Read the number of datasets. For each dataset, read the original triangle's side lengths and the candidate points in space.
2. **Generate Triplets**: For each combination of three distinct points, generate all permutations to consider all possible orderings of these points as vertices of the triangle (P, Q, R).
3. **Distance Calculation**: For each permutation, compute the distances between the points to form the sides QR, RP, and PQ.
4. **Ratio Check**: Calculate the ratios of the sides of the candidate triangle to the original triangle's sides. Check if these ratios are consistent within a 0.01% error margin.
5. **Validation**: If a valid permutation is found, output the indices of the points in the required order.
