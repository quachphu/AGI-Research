To solve this problem, we need to determine the equal share of pocket money each brother receives after combining their individual amounts and splitting the total equally. The solution involves a straightforward calculation to find the average of the combined amounts.

### Approach
1. **Input Reading**: Read the input values which represent the pocket money amounts received by Alice and Brown.
2. **Sum Calculation**: Compute the total amount of pocket money by adding the two values.
3. **Equal Division**: Divide the total amount by 2 to get the equal share for each brother.
4. **Output the Result**: Print the result of the division.

The key insight here is that the sum of two numbers divided by 2 gives the average, which is the equal share each brother receives. This approach ensures that we handle the input constraints efficiently and directly.

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    a, b = map(int, input().split())
    print((a + b) // 2)

main()
```

### Explanation
- **Reading Input**: The input is read as a single line containing two integers separated by a space. These integers are parsed into variables `a` and `b`.
- **Sum Calculation**: The sum of `a` and `b` is computed to get the total pocket money.
- **Division**: The total sum is divided by 2 using integer division to ensure the result is an integer, which represents the equal share each brother gets.
- **Output**: The result is printed, which is the amount each brother receives after splitting the total equally.

This approach efficiently computes the result in constant time, O(1), making it optimal for the given problem constraints.
