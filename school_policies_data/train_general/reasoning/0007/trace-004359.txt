Each operation involves splitting a cookie into two new cookies, and the goal is to choose the optimal splits to achieve the highest possible sum.

2. **Priority Queue (Max-Heap)**: Use a max-heap to always split the cookie that provides the maximum gain. This greedy approach ensures that each split contributes the highest possible value to the sum.
3. **Dynamic Handling of Splits**: For each test case, start with the initial cookie and repeatedly split the cookie with the highest gain until exactly N cookies are generated. The sum of the taste values is updated dynamically as each split is performed.
