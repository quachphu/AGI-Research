2. **Process Each Time Step**: For each step, check the poses of both players and update their attack power accordingly.
3. **Check for Fouls**: Determine if any player attacks with 0 attack power, which results in an immediate loss for that player unless both players do so.
4. **Check for Valid Attacks**: If both players attack with valid attack power (>=1), compare their attack powers to determine the winner. If one player attacks while the other is in a different pose (tameru or mamoru), apply the respective rules to determine the outcome.
5. **Update Attack Power**: After resolving each step, update the attack power for the next step based on the current poses.
