To solve this problem, we need to determine how many chocolate bars Alice and Bob consume when they start eating from opposite ends of a line of chocolate bars. Alice starts from the left and Bob from the right, and they cannot eat the same bar simultaneously. If they reach the same bar at the same time, Bob lets Alice take it.

### Approach
1. **Initialization**: Use two pointers, `a_ptr` starting at the leftmost bar (index 0) and `b_ptr` starting at the rightmost bar (index n-1). Track the cumulative time each has spent eating with `a_time` and `b_time`, and their respective counts of consumed bars with `a_count` and `b_count`.
2. **Simulation Loop**: Continue the loop while `a_ptr` is less than or equal to `b_ptr` to ensure all bars are considered.
3. **Time Comparison**: At each iteration, compare the cumulative times (`a_time` and `b_time`). The player with the smaller cumulative time will consume their next bar. If times are equal, Alice takes the next bar.
4. **Update Pointers and Times**: Adjust the pointers and times accordingly after each bar consumption. Move Alice's pointer to the right or Bob's pointer to the left based on who consumed the bar.

### Solution Code
```python
n = int(input())
t = list(map(int, input().split()))

a_ptr = 0
b_ptr = n - 1
a_time = 0
b_time = 0
a_count = 0
b_count = 0

while a_ptr <= b_ptr:
    if a_time <= b_time:
        a_time += t[a_ptr]
        a_count += 1
        a_ptr += 1
    else:
        b_time += t[b_ptr]
        b_count += 1
        b_ptr -= 1

print(a_count, b_count)
```

### Explanation
- **Initialization**: Start with pointers at both ends and cumulative times set to zero.
- **Loop Execution**: The loop runs as long as there are bars between the two pointers. In each iteration, the player with the smaller cumulative time (or Alice if times are equal) consumes the next bar.
- **Time and Pointer Updates**: After consuming a bar, the respective pointer is moved inward, and the consumed bar's time is added to the player's cumulative time. This ensures that the next bar to be consumed is determined based on the updated cumulative times.

This approach efficiently simulates the process, ensuring that each player consumes bars optimally according to their available time, leading to an accurate count of consumed bars for both Alice and Bob.
