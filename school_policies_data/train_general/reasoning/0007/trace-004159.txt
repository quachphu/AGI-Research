The solution involves understanding the geometric properties of the triangle and applying mathematical analysis to derive the required value.

### Approach
1. **Problem Analysis**: The given right triangle has vertices at (0, 0), (0, a), and (b, 0). The total area of the triangle is (a * b) / 2. We need to find the minimal x-value such that the area to the right of this x-value is at most p% of the total area.
2. **Geometric Insight**: The area to the right of x = val forms a smaller similar triangle. The ratio of the areas of similar triangles is the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides.
3. **Mathematical Formulation**: Using the properties of similar triangles, we derive that the required x-value (val) is given by `b * (1 - sqrt(p / 100))`. This formula is derived by setting the ratio of the areas to be at most p/100 and solving for val.

### Solution Code
```python
import math

a, b, p = map(int, input().split())
percentage = p / 100.0
sqrt_p = math.sqrt(percentage)
val = b * (1 - sqrt_p)
print("{0:.2f}".format(val))
```
