To solve this problem, we need to determine the number of ways to choose two balls such that the sum of their numbers is even. The solution leverages the mathematical properties of even and odd numbers and combinatorial selection.

### Approach
1. **Understanding Even and Odd Sums**:
   - The sum of two even numbers is even.
   - The sum of two odd numbers is even.
   - The sum of an even and an odd number is odd.
   
2. **Combinatorial Selection**:
   - The number of ways to choose 2 even balls from N even balls is given by the combination formula \( C(N, 2) = \frac{N \times (N-1)}{2} \).
   - Similarly, the number of ways to choose 2 odd balls from M odd balls is \( C(M, 2) = \frac{M \times (M-1)}{2} \).

3. **Summing Valid Combinations**:
   - The total valid pairs are the sum of combinations of two even balls and two odd balls.

### Solution Code
```python
n, m = map(int, input().split())
print((n * (n - 1) + m * (m - 1)) // 2)
```

### Explanation
- **Reading Input**: The input consists of two integers, N and M, representing the count of even and odd numbers respectively.
- **Calculating Combinations**:
  - For even numbers, compute \( C(N, 2) \) using \( \frac{N \times (N-1)}{2} \).
  - For odd numbers, compute \( C(M, 2) \) using \( \frac{M \times (M-1)}{2} \).
- **Summing Results**: Add the results of the two combinations to get the total number of valid pairs.
- **Output**: Print the computed result.

This approach efficiently computes the required number of pairs using combinatorial mathematics, ensuring optimal performance even for the upper constraint limits.
