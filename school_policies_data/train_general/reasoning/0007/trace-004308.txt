The price must be a positive integer, and if multiple prices satisfy the condition, we must return the lowest one. If no such price exists, we return -1.

### Approach
1. **Understanding Tax Calculation**: The tax amounts A and B are calculated by rounding down the product of the price and the tax rate. For a price X, the 8% tax is `floor(X * 0.08)` and the 10% tax is `floor(X * 0.10)`.

   - For the 8% tax rate (A), X must be in the interval `[ceil(A * 12.5), floor((A + 1) * 12.5 - 1e-9)]`.
   - For the 10% tax rate (B), X must be in the interval `[B * 10, (B + 1) * 10 - 1]`.
