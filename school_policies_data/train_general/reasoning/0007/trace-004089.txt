To solve this problem, we need to distribute scholarships among students based on their exam marks (3, 4, 5) while adhering to specific constraints. The goal is to minimize a function that measures the disparity between scholarships given to different groups of students.

### Approach
1. **Problem Analysis**:
   - Each student receives a scholarship based on their mark (3, 4, or 5), with the constraints that scholarships for the same mark are equal, and the amounts are non-decreasing (k3 ≤ k4 ≤ k5).
   - The total scholarship sum must exactly match the given budget `s`.
   - The function to minimize is the sum of squared differences between consecutive scholarship amounts.

2. **Key Insights**:
   - We need to explore possible values for k4 (scholarship for mark 4) and derive k3 and k5 (scholarships for marks 3 and 5) based on the remaining budget after allocating for k4.
   - Using the Extended Euclidean Algorithm helps efficiently solve the linear Diophantine equation derived from the budget constraints, allowing us to find valid integer solutions for k3 and k5.

3. **Algorithm**:
   - Iterate over possible values of k4.
   - For each k4, compute the remaining budget and check if it can be divided into valid scholarships for k3 and k5 using the Extended Euclidean Algorithm.
   - Check constraints for valid ranges of k3 and k5 and compute the minimization function for valid solutions.
