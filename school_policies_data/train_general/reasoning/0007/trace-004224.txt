2. **Early Termination for Sufficient Initial Likes**: If the initial likes (S) already meet or exceed the threshold (L), the person survives immediately.
3. **Handling Zero Growth**: If the growth factor (C) is zero, the likes will never increase, so the person can only survive if the initial likes meet the threshold.
4. **Exponential Growth Calculation**: For non-zero growth factors, compute the required growth using an optimized method to avoid overflow. This involves using exponentiation by squaring and early termination when the threshold is exceeded.

import sys
