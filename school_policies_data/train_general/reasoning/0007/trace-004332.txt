To solve this problem, we need to determine the smallest possible cutoff score for participants advancing to the final stage of an Olympiad based on their combined scores from two contests. The cutoff score is determined by the total score of the participant in the 100th position after both contests.

### Approach
1. **Understanding the Problem**: The problem requires us to find the minimum possible cutoff score such that at least 100 participants have a combined score equal to or higher than this cutoff. The constraints given are derived from the scores of participants in the top 100 of each contest.
2. **Key Insight**: Each participant in the top 100 of the first contest has a minimum score in the second contest, and vice versa. The cutoff score must be the maximum of two possible values:
   - The sum of the minimum score in the first contest and the minimum score in the second contest for the top 100 participants of the first contest.
   - The sum of the minimum score in the first contest and the minimum score in the second contest for the top 100 participants of the second contest.
3. **Formula**: The smallest possible cutoff score is the maximum of the sums \(a + b\) and \(c + d\), where \(a, b, c, d\) are the given inputs for each test case.

### Solution Code
```python
t = int(input())
for _ in range(t):
    a, b, c, d = map(int, input().split())
    res = max(a + b, c + d)
    print(res)
```

- **Reading Input**: The number of test cases \(t\) is read first. For each test case, the values \(a, b, c, d\) are read.
- **Compute Result**: For each test case, compute the maximum value between \(a + b\) (sum of the minimum scores from the first contest's top 100 participants) and \(c + d\) (sum of the minimum scores from the second contest's top 100 participants).
- **Output Result**: The result for each test case is printed, which represents the smallest possible cutoff score.

This approach efficiently computes the required cutoff score by leveraging the constraints provided and ensures the solution is optimal and runs in constant time for each test case.
