To solve this problem, we need to count Special Coprime numbers between two given numbers L and R (inclusive). A Special Coprime number is defined as a number where the sum of its digits and the sum of the squares of its digits are coprime (i.e., their greatest common divisor is 1).

### Approach
1. **Digit Dynamic Programming (DP)**: Given the constraints (L and R can be up to 10^18), a brute-force approach is infeasible. Instead, we use digit DP to efficiently count valid numbers in a given range.
2. **State Tracking**: Track the sum of digits (sum1), the sum of squares of digits (sum2), whether the current number is tight (adheres to the upper bound of the range), and whether the number has started (to handle leading zeros).
3. **Memoization**: Use memoization to avoid recalculating states, which significantly improves performance.
4. **Range Calculation**: For each test case, compute the count of valid numbers from 0 to R and subtract the count from 0 to L-1 to get the result for the range [L, R].
