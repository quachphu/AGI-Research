Monocarp serves the customer who arrived first (FIFO), while Polycarp serves the customer expected to spend the most money, with ties broken by arrival order. The solution must process up to 500,000 queries efficiently.

### Approach

   - **Deque (Double-ended Queue)**: To manage Monocarp's FIFO service. This helps efficiently pop from the front when serving the earliest unserved customer.
   - **Max-Heap**: To manage Polycarp's priority service. The heap stores tuples of negative money values (to simulate a max-heap using Python's min-heap) and customer indices.
   - **List**: To track whether each customer has been served.

2. **Handling Queries**:
   - **Type 1 (Customer Arrival)**: Add the customer to both the deque and the heap.
   - **Type 2 (Monocarp's Service)**: Serve the earliest unserved customer by dequeuing from the front of the deque, skipping already served customers.
   - **Type 3 (Polycarp's Service)**: Serve the customer with the highest expected spending by popping from the heap, skipping already served customers.

from heapq import heappush, heappop
from collections import deque

        if parts[0] == '1':

            heappush(heap_polycarp, (-m, customer_count))

                if not served[current - 1]:
