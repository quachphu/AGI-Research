To solve this problem, we need to determine the minimum number of moves required for N pieces to visit all M given coordinates on a number line. The key insight is to strategically place the pieces such that the total movement is minimized.

### Approach
1. **Sort the Coordinates**: First, sort the list of target coordinates. This helps in efficiently calculating the gaps between consecutive coordinates.
2. **Calculate Gaps**: Compute the gaps between each consecutive pair of sorted coordinates.
3. **Select Largest Gaps**: The problem can be reduced to selecting the largest (N-1) gaps. By splitting the sorted coordinates at these largest gaps, we minimize the total movement required.
4. **Sum of Movements**: The total movement is the difference between the maximum and minimum coordinates minus the sum of the largest (N-1) gaps.

### Solution Code
```python
n, m = map(int, input().split())
x = list(map(int, input().split()))

if n >= m:
    print(0)
else:
    x.sort()
    gaps = [x[i] - x[i-1] for i in range(1, m)]
    gaps.sort(reverse=True)
    sum_gaps = sum(gaps[:n-1])
    print(x[-1] - x[0] - sum_gaps)
```

### Explanation
1. **Sorting the Coordinates**: Sorting helps in efficiently determining the contiguous segments that each piece can cover.
2. **Gaps Calculation**: The gaps between consecutive sorted coordinates are computed to identify where the largest splits should be made.
3. **Optimal Splitting**: By selecting the largest (N-1) gaps, we ensure that the segments between these gaps are covered by different pieces, minimizing the total movement required.
4. **Total Movement Calculation**: The total movement is derived by subtracting the sum of the largest gaps from the total range of coordinates (from the smallest to the largest).

This approach ensures that we efficiently compute the minimal moves required by leveraging sorting and greedy selection of gaps, resulting in an optimal solution.
