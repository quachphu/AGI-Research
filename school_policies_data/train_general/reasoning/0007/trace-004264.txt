To solve this problem, we need to determine the winner of a game played by Alice and Brown with two piles of stones. The game rules allow players to take a certain number of stones from one pile and transfer some to the other pile. The player who cannot make a move loses. The solution involves analyzing the difference between the number of stones in the two piles to decide the winner.

### Approach
The key insight is that the winner can be determined by the absolute difference between the number of stones in the two piles. If the absolute difference is 2 or more, Alice (the first player) can always make a move that forces Brown into a losing position. Conversely, if the absolute difference is less than 2, Brown will win because Alice cannot make a move that leaves Brown in a losing position.

### Solution Code
```python
x, y = map(int, input().split())
if abs(x - y) >= 2:
    print("Alice")
else:
    print("Brown")
```

### Explanation
1. **Problem Analysis**: The game is determined by the players' ability to make moves based on the current state of the piles. Each move reduces the total number of stones, but the critical factor is the difference between the two piles.
2. **Key Insight**: If the difference between the two piles is 2 or more, the current player can always make a move that forces the next player into a position where the difference is less than 2, which is a losing position. If the difference is less than 2, the current player cannot make such a move, leading to their loss.
3. **Algorithm**: The solution checks the absolute difference between the two piles. If it is 2 or more, Alice wins; otherwise, Brown wins. This approach runs in constant time, O(1), as it involves simple arithmetic operations and a conditional check.

This solution efficiently determines the winner by leveraging the difference between the piles, ensuring optimal performance even for the upper constraint values.
