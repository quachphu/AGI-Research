Both the cone and the cylinder have the same radius and height, which is denoted by 'r' for each jug. The radius 'r' varies from 1 to 'n' (given as input).

### Approach
1. **Understanding the Geometry**: Each jug consists of a cylinder and a cone. The volume of a cylinder is given by \( \pi r^2 h \) and the volume of a cone is \( \frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h \). Here, both the radius and height for both shapes are 'r', so the total volume for each jug is the sum of the volumes of the cylinder and the cone.
2. **Volume Calculation**: For a jug with radius 'r', the volume is calculated as \( \pi r^3 + \frac{1}{3} \pi r^3 = \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3 \).
3. **Summing Volumes**: The cumulative volume for all jugs from radius 1 to 'n' is the sum of \( \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3 \) for each r from 1 to n. This sum can be simplified using the formula for the sum of cubes, which is \( \left( \frac{n(n+1)}{2} \right)^2 \).
4. **Formula Simplification**: Substituting the sum of cubes into the volume formula gives the total volume as \( \frac{\pi n^2 (n+1)^2}{3} \).

```python
import math

```

2. **Calculating Total Volume**: Using the derived formula \( \frac{\pi n^2 (n+1)^2}{3} \), we compute the total volume for all jugs from radius 1 to 'n'.
3. **Rounding and Printing**: The computed volume is rounded to the nearest integer and printed as the result.

This approach efficiently computes the cumulative volume using a direct mathematical formula, ensuring optimal performance even for large values of 'n'.
