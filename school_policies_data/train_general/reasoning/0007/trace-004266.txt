To solve this problem, we need to determine the shortest bus route between two stops on a bus route that includes both a linear segment and a circular loop. The bus can move in two directions from stops 1 to 5, but once it enters the circular loop (stops 5 to 9), it follows a fixed one-way path. Our goal is to find the shortest path between any two stops, considering both possible directions from stops 1 to 5.

### Approach
1. **Understand the Route Structure**: The bus route consists of a linear segment (stops 0 to 5) and a circular loop (5 → 6 → 7 → 8 → 9 → 5). The bus can move left (towards 0) or right (towards 5) from stops 1 to 5. Once it reaches 5, it can either continue into the circular loop or turn back towards 0.
2. **Generate Paths**: For each possible direction from the starting stop, generate the path until the destination stop is reached. This involves:
   - Moving left towards 0 and then right towards 5 if necessary.
   - Moving right towards 5 and then entering the circular loop.
   - Handling the fixed circular loop for stops 6 to 9.
3. **Compare Paths**: For stops 1 to 5, generate both left and right paths, then choose the shortest one. If paths are of equal length, prefer the direction that reaches the destination first.

        path.append(current)
        if current == end:
            return path

for _ in range(n):
    start, end = map(int, input().split())

        right = generate_right_path(start, end)
        if len(left) < len(right):
            path = left

            path = right
