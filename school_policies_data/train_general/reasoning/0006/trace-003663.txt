To solve this problem, we need to determine the winner of a coding contest based on the points accumulated by participants over multiple rounds. The winner is the participant with the highest points at the end of all rounds. In case of a tie, the winner is the participant who reached the highest score first during the rounds.

### Approach
1. **Track Points Over Rounds**: For each participant, track their cumulative points after each round they participate in. This helps in determining when they first reached the maximum score.
2. **Determine Maximum Score**: After processing all rounds, compute the final score for each participant and identify the maximum score.
3. **Identify Candidates**: Collect all participants who have the maximum final score.
4. **Determine Earliest Achievement**: For each candidate, determine the earliest round in which they first reached or exceeded the maximum score. The participant who achieved this earliest is declared the winner.

### Solution Code
```python
n = int(input())
participants = {}

for round_num in range(1, n + 1):
    name, pts = input().split()
    pts = int(pts)
    if name not in participants:
        participants[name] = {'current': 0, 'history': []}
    participants[name]['current'] += pts
    participants[name]['history'].append((participants[name]['current'], round_num))

final_scores = {name: data['current'] for name, data in participants.items()}
max_score = max(final_scores.values())
candidates = [name for name, score in final_scores.items() if score == max_score]

winner = None
min_round = float('inf')

for name in candidates:
    history = participants[name]['history']
    for score, rnd in history:
        if score >= max_score:
            if rnd < min_round:
                min_round = rnd
                winner = name
            break  # first occurrence is the earliest

print(winner)
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input and Tracking Points**: The input is read and processed round by round. For each participant, their cumulative score is updated and stored along with the round number in their history.
2. **Final Scores and Maximum Calculation**: After processing all rounds, the final scores of all participants are determined. The maximum score among these final scores is identified.
3. **Candidate Identification**: Participants who have the maximum score are identified as candidates for the winner.
4. **Earliest Achievement Check**: For each candidate, their history of scores is checked to find the earliest round where they reached or exceeded the maximum score. The participant with the earliest such round is selected as the winner.

This approach ensures that we efficiently track the necessary data and determine the winner based on the given criteria, handling both the points accumulation and tie-breaking scenarios correctly.
