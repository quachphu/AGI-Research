To solve this problem, we need to optimize the debts among a group of friends such that the total sum of debts is minimized while preserving the net balance for each person. The net balance for each person is the difference between the total money they should give and the total money they should take.

### Approach
1. **Calculate Net Balances**: For each person, compute the net balance by considering all debts they owe and are owed. This is done by subtracting the amount they owe from their balance and adding the amount they are owed to their balance.
2. **Sum Absolute Values**: The minimum total debt after optimization is derived by summing the absolute values of all net balances and dividing by two. This works because each debt transaction affects two people, and the absolute values ensure we account for both creditors and debtors.

### Solution Code
```python
n, m = map(int, input().split())
net = [0] * (n + 1)  # 1-based indexing for n people

for _ in range(m):
    a, b, c = map(int, input().split())
    net[a] -= c
    net[b] += c

total_debts = sum(abs(x) for x in net[1:]) // 2
print(total_debts)
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The input consists of the number of people (n) and the number of debts (m). Each subsequent line details a debt from one person to another.
2. **Computing Net Balances**: For each debt, the debtor's balance is decreased by the debt amount, and the creditor's balance is increased by the same amount.
3. **Summing Absolute Values**: The absolute values of all net balances are summed to account for both debtors and creditors. Dividing this sum by two gives the minimum total debt, as each transaction is counted twice in the sum of absolute values (once for the debtor and once for the creditor).

This approach efficiently computes the minimum possible total debt by consolidating transactions, ensuring the net effect on each person's balance remains unchanged while minimizing the total sum of debts.
