### Approach
1. **Understanding Blocks**: A block is a maximal sequence of consecutive identical digits that cannot be extended in either direction. For example, in the number 00027734000, the blocks are of lengths 3, 2, 1, and 3.

2. **Formulating the Problem**: For each length i from 1 to n, count how many times a block of exactly i digits appears across all n-digit numbers. This requires considering the constraints on the digits before and after the block.

3. **Efficient Calculation**:
   - **Precompute Powers of 10**: Compute powers of 10 modulo 998244353 up to the required exponent to avoid repeated calculations.
   - **Mathematical Formula**: Use a formula to compute the number of blocks for each length i. For each i, the formula considers three cases: blocks starting at the beginning, middle, and end of the number.

### Solution Code

MOD = 998244353

n = int(input())

if n == 1:
    print(10)

    pow10 = [1] * n  # pow10[e] = 10^e mod MOD, for e up to n-2
    for e in range(1, n):
