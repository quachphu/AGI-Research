To solve this problem, we need to determine the minimum area of a square land that can fit two identical rectangular houses with dimensions \(a \times b\). The rectangles can be rotated, and their sides must be parallel to the sides of the square.

### Approach
The key insight is to recognize that the optimal arrangement of the two rectangles can be either:
1. Placed side by side along their shorter side, resulting in a combined width of \(2 \times \text{min}(a, b)\) and height of \(\text{max}(a, b)\).
2. Rotated such that their longer side is considered, but this arrangement is less optimal compared to the first.

The minimum side length of the square will be the maximum value between twice the shorter side of the rectangle and the longer side of the rectangle. This approach ensures that both rectangles fit within the square without overlapping.

### Solution Code
```python
import sys

def minimal_square_area():
    t = int(sys.stdin.readline())
    for _ in range(t):
        a, b = map(int, sys.stdin.readline().split())
        m = min(a, b)
        M = max(a, b)
        side = max(2 * m, M)
        print(side * side)

minimal_square_area()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input:** The input consists of multiple test cases. For each test case, we read the dimensions \(a\) and \(b\) of the rectangles.
2. **Determine Minimum and Maximum Sides:** Calculate the minimum (m) and maximum (M) of the two dimensions.
3. **Calculate Square Side:** The side length of the square is determined by the maximum value between twice the minimum dimension (to fit both rectangles side by side) and the maximum dimension (to ensure the height or width fits).
4. **Output the Result:** The area of the square is the square of the computed side length.

This approach efficiently computes the minimum possible area of the square by considering the optimal arrangement of the rectangles, ensuring the solution is both correct and optimal.
