### Approach
1. **Data Structure**: Use a Disjoint Set Union (DSU) with path compression and union by size to manage dynamic connectivity. Each node in the DSU will track the parity (0 for synonym, 1 for antonym) relative to its root.
2. **Union-Find with Parity**: For each word, the DSU will track its parent and the parity (XOR sum) from the node to its parent. This helps in determining if two words are synonyms or antonyms based on their paths to the root.
3. **Handling Relations**: For each new relation, determine if it creates a contradiction. If valid, merge the sets with appropriate parity adjustments.
4. **Query Processing**: After processing all relations, determine the relationship between queried words using their root and parity values.
