To solve this problem, we need to determine the direction a hero should take from point B to reach point C, given that the hero is facing away from point A. The possible directions are left, right, or straight. The solution involves calculating the relative direction using vector cross products.

### Approach
1. **Understand the Hero's Direction**: The hero is at point B, facing away from point A. This means the hero's direction vector is from point A to point B (vector AB).
2. **Compute Vectors**: Calculate the vectors AB (from A to B) and BC (from B to C).
3. **Cross Product Calculation**: Use the cross product of vectors AB and BC to determine the relative direction. The cross product helps identify if the direction to C is a left turn, right turn, or straight ahead based on the sign of the result.

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    xa, ya = map(int, input().split())
    xb, yb = map(int, input().split())
    xc, yc = map(int, input().split())
    
    dx_ab = xb - xa
    dy_ab = yb - ya
    dx_bc = xc - xb
    dy_bc = yc - yb
    
    cross = dx_ab * dy_bc - dy_ab * dx_bc
    
    if cross == 0:
        print("TOWARDS")
    elif cross > 0:
        print("LEFT")
    else:
        print("RIGHT")

main()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The coordinates of points A, B, and C are read from the input.
2. **Vector Calculation**: Compute the vectors AB and BC using the coordinates of the points.
3. **Cross Product**: The cross product of vectors AB and BC is calculated. The sign of the cross product determines the direction:
   - **Zero**: Indicates vectors are collinear, so the direction is straight ("TOWARDS").
   - **Positive**: Indicates a left turn.
   - **Negative**: Indicates a right turn.

This approach efficiently determines the required direction using vector mathematics, ensuring the solution is both optimal and easy to understand.
