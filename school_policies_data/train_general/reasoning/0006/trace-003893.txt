2. **Summing Exponents**: For each digit in the input number a, sum the exponents of the prime factors of its factorial. This gives the total exponents for primes 2, 3, 5, and 7.
3. **Recursive Backtracking with Memoization**: Using a recursive approach with memoization, we explore all possible digits from 9 down to 2 to construct the largest possible number. We check if using a digit is feasible given the current prime exponents and recursively solve the remaining problem. The solution is memoized to avoid redundant calculations.
