To solve this problem, we need to determine the minimum and maximum number of Div2 rounds that Sereja could have missed before his current round. The key is to analyze the known rounds Sereja participated in and their simultaneous rounds, then use the remaining identifiers to compute the possible missed rounds.

### Approach
1. **Input Reading and Initialization**: Read the current round identifier and the number of previous rounds Sereja participated in. Collect all known identifiers from these rounds, including any simultaneous rounds.
2. **Identify Unknown Rounds**: Generate a list of all identifiers less than the current round that are not in the known set.
3. **Group Consecutive Unknown Identifiers**: Split the unknown identifiers into consecutive intervals. Each interval represents a block of numbers that can be analyzed for potential Div2 rounds.
4. **Calculate Minimum and Maximum Missed Rounds**:
   - **Minimum Missed Rounds**: Maximize the number of pairs (Div2 and Div1 rounds) to minimize the count of Div2 rounds. Each pair contributes one Div2 round.
   - **Maximum Missed Rounds**: Treat each unknown identifier as a standalone Div2 round to maximize the count.
