2. **Classify Breaks:** Determine each break's orientation (vertical or horizontal) and its coordinates.

4. **Calculate Areas:** After all breaks are processed, compute the areas of the resulting rectangles and sort them in increasing order.

        # Horizontal break
        xmin = min(x1, x2)
        xmax = max(x1, x2)
        breaks.append(('h', y1, xmin, xmax))
