To solve this problem, we need to determine the maximum number of soldiers that can be killed in three turns of the game "GAGA: Go And Go Again" where Valera and Arcady take turns attacking each other's armies. The solution involves understanding the optimal strategy to maximize the total number of soldiers killed over the three turns.

### Approach
The key insight is to recognize that the optimal strategy involves each player coordinating their attacks to maximize the total number of deaths, even if it means sacrificing some of their own soldiers. By analyzing the turns step-by-step, we can derive a formula that gives the maximum number of soldiers killed:

1. **Valera's First Turn**: Valera can kill up to half of Arcady's soldiers, leaving the other half for future turns.
2. **Arcady's Turn**: Arcady, with the remaining soldiers, can kill the same number of Valera's soldiers.
3. **Valera's Second Turn**: Valera's remaining soldiers can then kill the remaining soldiers of Arcady.

This results in a total of \( \frac{3n}{2} \) soldiers killed, where \( n \) is the initial number of soldiers in each army. This formula works because each player's turns are structured to maximize the number of kills possible in each subsequent turn.

### Solution Code
```python
n = int(input())
print(3 * n // 2)
```

### Explanation
1. **Valera's First Turn**: Valera kills half of Arcady's soldiers, leaving \( \frac{n}{2} \) soldiers.
2. **Arcady's Turn**: Arcady uses his remaining \( \frac{n}{2} \) soldiers to kill \( \frac{n}{2} \) of Valera's soldiers.
3. **Valera's Second Turn**: Valera's remaining \( \frac{n}{2} \) soldiers kill the remaining \( \frac{n}{2} \) of Arcady's soldiers.

Summing these kills gives \( \frac{n}{2} + \frac{n}{2} + \frac{n}{2} = \frac{3n}{2} \), which is the maximum possible number of soldiers killed in three turns. This approach ensures that each turn's potential is fully utilized, leading to the optimal solution.
