### Approach
1. **Identify Midpoint Cell**: Find a cell (r, c) on the line r + c = n + 1 such that there is a valid path from (1, 1) to (r, c) and from (r, c) to (n, n). This is done by iterating through possible cells and using queries to check connectivity.

3. **Concatenate Paths**: Combine the two sub-paths to form the complete path from (1, 1) to (n, n).
