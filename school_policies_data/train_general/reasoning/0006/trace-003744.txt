To solve this problem, we need to determine the time each student gets off the bus after being transported from a bus stop to their respective destinations. The bus operates in batches, picking up students in groups of up to `m` passengers and transporting them to their destinations. The bus stops at each destination along the way, and the time taken for students to get off depends on the number of students exiting at each stop.

### Approach
1. **Group Students into Batches**: Process students in batches of up to `m` students each. Each batch departs when the bus is available and all students in the batch have arrived.
2. **Track Bus Departure Time**: For each batch, the departure time is determined by the maximum of the bus's return time from the previous trip and the arrival time of the last student in the current batch.
3. **Simulate Bus Trips**: For each batch, the bus travels to the farthest destination, stopping at each intermediate destination. The time taken to reach each destination and the time students take to exit the bus are calculated.
4. **Calculate Return Time**: After dropping off all students in a batch, the bus returns to the stop, and the time taken for this return trip is added to determine when the bus will be available for the next batch.
