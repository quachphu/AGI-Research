To solve this problem, we need to determine the amount of water remaining in Bottle 2 after transferring as much water as possible from Bottle 2 to Bottle 1. The solution involves calculating the maximum amount of water that can be transferred and then subtracting this amount from the initial quantity in Bottle 2.

### Approach
1. **Understand the Problem**: We have two bottles. Bottle 1 has a maximum capacity of A milliliters and currently contains B milliliters. Bottle 2 contains C milliliters. We need to transfer as much water as possible from Bottle 2 to Bottle 1 until Bottle 1 is full or Bottle 2 is empty.
2. **Calculate Available Space**: The space available in Bottle 1 is given by \( A - B \), where A is the capacity and B is the current amount of water in Bottle 1.
3. **Determine Transfer Amount**: The maximum amount of water that can be transferred from Bottle 2 to Bottle 1 is the minimum of the available space in Bottle 1 and the current amount of water in Bottle 2. This is represented as \( \text{min}(A - B, C) \).
4. **Compute Remaining Water**: Subtract the transferred amount from the initial quantity in Bottle 2 to get the remaining water in Bottle 2.

### Solution Code
```python
a, b, c = map(int, input().split())
pour = min(a - b, c)
print(c - pour)
```

### Explanation
- **Input Reading**: The code reads three integers from the input which represent the capacity of Bottle 1 (A), the current water in Bottle 1 (B), and the current water in Bottle 2 (C).
- **Calculate Transfer**: The amount of water that can be poured from Bottle 2 to Bottle 1 is determined by the minimum of the available space in Bottle 1 (A - B) and the current water in Bottle 2 (C).
- **Compute Remaining Water**: Subtract the transferred amount from the initial amount in Bottle 2 to get the remaining water, which is then printed.

This approach efficiently calculates the result using basic arithmetic operations, ensuring optimal performance and correctness for the given constraints.
