3. **Determine Remaining '?'**: Calculate the number of remaining '?' characters, considering pairs and a possible middle character in case of odd-length strings.

4. **Check Feasibility**: Ensure the required counts of '0's and '1's can be met with the remaining '?' characters. Check for valid parity (even/odd) based on the presence of a middle character.

5. **Assign Values to '?'**: Replace the remaining '?' characters with '0's and '1's to meet the exact counts while maintaining the palindrome structure.
