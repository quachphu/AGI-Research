To solve this problem, we need to determine how many participants will advance to the next round of a contest based on their scores. The participants advance if their score is at least as high as the k-th place finisher's score and is positive.

### Approach
1. **Input Reading**: Read the number of participants `n` and the position `k` which determines the cutoff score.
2. **Determine Cutoff Score**: The cutoff score is the score of the participant in the k-th position. Since the scores are given in non-increasing order, the k-th position score is at index `k-1` (0-based indexing).
3. **Count Eligible Participants**: Iterate through all scores and count how many participants have scores greater than or equal to the cutoff score and also have a positive score.

### Solution Code
```python
n, k = map(int, input().split())
scores = list(map(int, input().split()))
cutoff = scores[k-1]
count = 0
for score in scores:
    if score >= cutoff and score > 0:
        count += 1
print(count)
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The first line of input contains two integers `n` and `k`. The second line contains `n` space-separated integers representing the scores of the participants in non-increasing order.
2. **Cutoff Score**: The score at the k-th position (index `k-1` in the list) is identified as the cutoff score.
3. **Eligibility Check**: For each score, check if it is greater than or equal to the cutoff score and also positive. If both conditions are met, increment the count of eligible participants.
4. **Output the Result**: Print the total count of participants who meet the criteria to advance to the next round.

This approach efficiently checks each participant's score against the cutoff and ensures only valid scores (positive and meeting the cutoff) are counted, providing the correct result in linear time, which is optimal given the problem constraints.
