To solve this problem, we need to determine the price of a single unreadable book from a receipt, given the total price of 10 books and the prices of the other 9 books (with one price marked as zero).

### Approach
1. **Read Input**: The first line of input contains the total price of all 10 books. The subsequent 10 lines contain the prices of the books, with one of these lines being zero (indicating the unreadable price).
2. **Calculate Sum of Given Prices**: Sum the prices of the 10 books read from the input, including the zero.
3. **Determine Missing Price**: Subtract the sum of the 10 prices (including zero) from the total price to find the missing price. This works because the zero in the sum of the 10 prices effectively means we are only summing the 9 known prices, and subtracting this from the total gives the missing price.

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    total = int(input())
    sum_books = sum(int(input()) for _ in range(10))
    print(total - sum_books)

main()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The first line is read as the total price of all 10 books. The next 10 lines are read as individual book prices.
2. **Sum Calculation**: The sum of these 10 values (including the zero) is computed. Since one of the values is zero, this sum effectively represents the sum of the 9 known prices.
3. **Finding the Missing Price**: By subtracting the computed sum (of the 9 known prices and zero) from the total price, we get the value of the missing book price. This is because the total price is the sum of all 10 books, so replacing the zero with the missing price and subtracting the sum of the 9 known prices gives the missing value directly.

This approach efficiently computes the missing price using straightforward arithmetic operations, ensuring an optimal solution with a time complexity of O(1) for a fixed number of inputs (10 books).
