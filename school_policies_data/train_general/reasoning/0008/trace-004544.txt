   - For each bit set in K, generate a new mask by flipping that bit to 0 and setting all lower bits to 1. This helps in exploring all possible values that are less than or equal to K.

   - For each candidate mask, compute the sum of utilities of all integers whose binary representation is a subset of the mask. This ensures that the bitwise OR of the selected integers will not exceed the mask, which in turn is less than or equal to K.
