### Approach
1. **Breadth-First Search (BFS)**: We use BFS to explore the shortest path from the initial position of the hexerpent's head to the goal position. Each state in the BFS represents the positions of all sections of the hexerpent.
2. **State Validity Checks**: For each state transition, we check if the new positions of the hexerpent's sections are valid, considering rocks, body structure constraints, and adjacency rules.
3. **Movement Constraints**: The hexerpent can move multiple non-adjacent sections at each step. Each section's movement must ensure the body remains connected and no new adjacent pairs are formed between non-consecutive sections.
