2. **Iterate Over Possible Euro Amounts**: Since euros must be multiples of 5, we iterate through all possible euro amounts (0, 5, 10, ...) up to the maximum possible value that does not exceed the given rubles.
3. **Calculate Maximum Dollars for Each Euro Amount**: For each euro amount, compute the remaining rubles and determine the maximum dollar amount that can be exchanged with the remaining rubles.
4. **Track the Maximum Exchange Value**: Keep track of the maximum total rubles exchanged (dollars + euros) and compute the minimum remaining rubles by subtracting this maximum from the initial amount.

### Solution Code

max_sum = 0

    rubles_used_e = e * E
