To solve this problem, we need to determine the minimum time required for all people to travel from City 1 to City 6 using a series of transports with varying capacities. The key insight is that the slowest transport (the one with the smallest capacity) will be the bottleneck, dictating the overall time required.

### Approach
1. **Identify the Bottleneck**: The transport with the smallest capacity will determine the slowest part of the journey. This is because it takes the longest time to move all people through this transport.
2. **Calculate Time for Bottleneck Transport**: The time required to move all people through the bottleneck transport is given by the ceiling of the division of the number of people by the bottleneck capacity. This can be computed efficiently using integer arithmetic.
3. **Total Time Calculation**: The total time includes the time taken by the bottleneck transport plus the fixed travel time of 5 minutes (one minute for each of the five cities).

### Solution Code
```python
n = int(input())
a = int(input())
b = int(input())
c = int(input())
d = int(input())
e = int(input())

x_min = min(a, b, c, d, e)
ans = (n + x_min - 1) // x_min + 4
print(ans)
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The code reads the number of people (N) and the capacities of the five transports (A, B, C, D, E).
2. **Finding the Bottleneck**: The minimum capacity among the transports is identified using the `min` function.
3. **Time Calculation**: The time required to move all people through the bottleneck transport is calculated using the formula `(N + x_min - 1) // x_min`, which effectively computes the ceiling of the division. Adding 4 accounts for the fixed travel time of 5 minutes (since the formula `ceil(N/x_min) + 4` is equivalent to `ceil(N/x_min) - 1 + 5`, where 5 is the sum of the travel times between cities).

This approach ensures that we efficiently determine the minimum time required by focusing on the critical bottleneck transport, leading to an optimal solution.
