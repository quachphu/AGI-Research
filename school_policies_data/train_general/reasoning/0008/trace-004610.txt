### Approach
1. **Conflict Detection**: For each pair of subjects, determine if there exists any pair of days where the scores of one subject are increasing while the other are decreasing. This creates a conflict graph where edges represent conflicting subjects.
2. **Complement Graph**: Convert the conflict graph into its complement, where edges represent non-conflicting subjects.
3. **Maximum Clique**: Find the maximum clique in the complement graph, which corresponds to the largest set of subjects that can be non-decreasing in the same permutation of days.
