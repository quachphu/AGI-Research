### Approach
1. **Understanding the Function**: The function \( F(x) = 2^x - 1 \) generates numbers known as Mersenne numbers. The problem requires finding the common prime factors of \( F(a) \) and \( F(b) \).
2. **Mathematical Insight**: The greatest common divisor (GCD) of \( F(a) \) and \( F(b) \) is \( F(\text{gcd}(a, b)) \). Thus, we need to find the number of distinct prime factors of \( F(\text{gcd}(a, b)) \).
3. **Precomputation**: Since \( a \) and \( b \) can be up to 23, we precompute the number of distinct prime factors for \( F(g) \) where \( g \) ranges from 1 to 23. This allows us to quickly answer each query by looking up the precomputed values.
