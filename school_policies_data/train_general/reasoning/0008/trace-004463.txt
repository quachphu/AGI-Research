To solve this problem, we need to determine the minimum total length of tape required to cover all broken segments on a stick using at most k pieces of tape. The key insight is to strategically split the tape at the largest gaps between consecutive broken segments to minimize the total length.

### Approach
1. **Identify Gaps**: Calculate the gaps between consecutive broken segments. These gaps represent the number of non-broken segments between each pair of broken segments.
2. **Sort Gaps**: Sort these gaps in descending order to prioritize the largest gaps for splitting.
3. **Sum Largest Gaps**: Sum the largest (k-1) gaps. This sum represents the maximum reduction in tape length achievable by splitting the tape into k pieces.
4. **Calculate Total Length**: The initial total length required to cover all segments in one piece is the distance from the first to the last broken segment. Subtract the sum of the largest (k-1) gaps from this initial length to get the minimum total length of tape required.

### Solution Code
```python
n, m, k = map(int, input().split())
broken = list(map(int, input().split()))

if k >= n:
    print(n)
else:
    gaps = []
    for i in range(1, n):
        gaps.append(broken[i] - broken[i-1] - 1)
    gaps.sort(reverse=True)
    sum_gaps = sum(gaps[:k-1])
    total = (broken[-1] - broken[0] + 1) - sum_gaps
    print(total)
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The input values are read, including the number of broken segments, the stick length, the maximum number of tape pieces allowed, and the positions of the broken segments.
2. **Edge Case Handling**: If the number of allowed tape pieces (k) is greater than or equal to the number of broken segments (n), each broken segment can be covered individually, resulting in a total length of n.
3. **Gap Calculation**: For each consecutive pair of broken segments, compute the gap (non-broken segments between them).
4. **Sorting Gaps**: The gaps are sorted in descending order to identify where the largest savings can be made by splitting the tape.
5. **Summing Largest Gaps**: The sum of the largest (k-1) gaps is calculated. These gaps are the optimal points to split the tape into k pieces.
6. **Total Length Calculation**: The initial length required to cover all segments in one piece is adjusted by subtracting the sum of the largest gaps, resulting in the minimum total length of tape needed.

This approach efficiently minimizes the tape length by leveraging the largest gaps between broken segments, ensuring optimal coverage with the allowed number of tape pieces.
