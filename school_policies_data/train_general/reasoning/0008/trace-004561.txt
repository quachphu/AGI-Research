The key insight is to leverage the intersection properties of rectangles and efficiently compute the maximum and minimum boundaries for each axis when excluding each rectangle one by one.

### Approach
1. **Precompute Boundaries**: For each rectangle, precompute the left and right maximum values for the x and y lower boundaries (x1, y1) and the left and right minimum values for the x and y upper boundaries (x2, y2). These precomputed values help in efficiently determining the intersection boundaries when excluding any single rectangle.
2. **Exclusion Check**: For each rectangle, compute the intersection boundaries of the remaining (n-1) rectangles. If the intersection is valid (i.e., the lower boundary is less than or equal to the upper boundary for both x and y axes), then any point within this intersection is a valid solution.
