### Approach
1. **Understanding Connectivity**: For an agency K to be valid, the shuttles must form a connected graph allowing any boarding point to reach point 1. This happens when K and N are coprime (i.e., their greatest common divisor (GCD) is 1).
2. **Euler's Totient Function**: The number of valid agencies is given by Euler's totient function φ(N), which counts the integers from 1 to N-1 that are coprime with N. This function can be computed efficiently using prime factorization.
