4. **Bitwise Operations**: Using properties of XOR and bitwise manipulation, we determine valid values for `A1 - k` and `A2 + k` that satisfy the conditions. This involves checking if `s - t` is even and non-negative, and ensuring the resulting values fall within valid ranges.
