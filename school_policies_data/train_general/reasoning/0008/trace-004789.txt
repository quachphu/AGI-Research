To solve this problem, we need to calculate the sum of all integers from 1 to n, but subtract all powers of two instead of adding them. The solution involves efficiently computing the sum of integers up to n and adjusting it by subtracting twice the sum of all powers of two up to n.

### Approach
1. **Sum of Integers from 1 to n**: The sum of the first n natural numbers can be computed using the formula \( \text{sum\_total} = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2} \).
2. **Sum of Powers of Two up to n**: The sum of all powers of two up to the largest power of two less than or equal to n can be derived using the formula \( 2^{k+1} - 1 \), where \( 2^k \) is the largest power of two less than or equal to n. This can be efficiently found using bit manipulation.
3. **Adjusting the Sum**: Subtract twice the sum of powers of two from the total sum of integers to get the final result.

### Solution Code
```python
t = int(input())
for _ in range(t):
    n = int(input())
    total = n * (n + 1) // 2
    k = n.bit_length() - 1
    pow_sum = (1 << (k + 1)) - 1
    res = total - 2 * pow_sum
    print(res)
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The number of test cases `t` is read first, followed by each value of `n`.
2. **Sum of Integers**: For each `n`, compute the sum of the first `n` integers using the formula \( \frac{n(n + 1)}{2} \).
3. **Largest Power of Two**: Determine the largest power of two less than or equal to `n` using bit length. The bit length of `n` gives the number of bits required to represent `n` in binary, and subtracting 1 gives the exponent of the largest power of two.
4. **Sum of Powers of Two**: Compute the sum of all powers of two up to the largest power using \( 2^{k+1} - 1 \).
5. **Adjusting the Total**: Subtract twice the sum of powers of two from the total sum to get the final result, which is then printed.

This approach efficiently handles large values of `n` up to \( 10^9 \) by leveraging bit manipulation and mathematical properties, ensuring the solution is both optimal and correct.
