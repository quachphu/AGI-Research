To solve this problem, we need to calculate the maximum flow in a network with two parts (A and B) and handle dynamic updates to the capacities of edges in part A. The solution involves efficiently maintaining the maximum flow value after each update.

### Approach
1. **Network Structure**: The network consists of two chains (A and B) and edges from A to B. The maximum flow is determined by the minimum capacities along paths through these chains and edges from A to B.
2. **Precompute B_min**: For each node in part B, compute the minimum capacity of the path from that node to the end of the B chain.
3. **Edge Contributions**: For each edge from A to B, determine its contribution to the maximum flow as the minimum of the path capacity in A up to that node, the edge's capacity, and the minimum path capacity in B from the target node to the end.
4. **Dynamic Updates**: Use a segment tree to maintain the prefix minimum capacities of the A chain efficiently. This allows us to quickly update the capacities and recompute the maximum flow after each query.
