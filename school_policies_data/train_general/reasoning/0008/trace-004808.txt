To solve this problem, we need to determine the cost for Takahashi to ride a Ferris wheel based on his age. The cost structure is tiered: full price for those 13 years or older, half price for children between 6 and 12 years old, and free for children 5 years old or younger.

### Approach
1. **Input Reading**: Read the input values for Takahashi's age (A) and the full cost (B).
2. **Condition Check**: Based on Takahashi's age:
   - If he is 13 years or older, the cost is the full price (B yen).
   - If he is between 6 and 12 years old (inclusive), the cost is half of B yen.
   - If he is 5 years old or younger, the cost is free (0 yen).
3. **Output the Result**: Print the calculated cost based on the age condition.

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    A, B = map(int, input().split())
    if A >= 13:
        print(B)
    elif A >= 6:
        print(B // 2)
    else:
        print(0)

main()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The input values are read and converted into integers using `map(int, input().split())`.
2. **Conditional Checks**:
   - The first condition checks if Takahashi is 13 or older. If true, the cost is the full price B.
   - The second condition checks if he is between 6 and 12 years old. If true, the cost is half of B, which is computed using integer division to ensure the result is an integer.
   - The final condition handles the case where he is 5 years old or younger, resulting in a cost of 0 yen.
3. **Output**: The result is printed based on the evaluated condition.

This approach efficiently determines the correct cost using straightforward conditional checks, ensuring clarity and correctness given the problem constraints.
