### Approach

   - Yellow ball: 2 yellow crystals each.
   - Green ball: 1 yellow and 1 blue crystal each.

2. **Calculate Required Crystals**:
   - **Yellow Crystals**: Needed for yellow balls (2 per ball) and green balls (1 per ball).
   - **Blue Crystals**: Needed for green balls (1 per ball) and blue balls (3 per ball).

3. **Determine Additional Crystals**:

   - Subtract the available crystals from the required crystals to find the deficit for each type.
   - Sum the deficits to get the total additional crystals needed.

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    import sys
    A, B = map(int, sys.stdin.readline().split())
    x, y, z = map(int, sys.stdin.readline().split())
    required_yellow = 2 * x + y
    required_blue = y + 3 * z
    additional_yellow = max(0, required_yellow - A)
    additional_blue = max(0, required_blue - B)
    print(additional_yellow + additional_blue)

main()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The code reads the available yellow (A) and blue (B) crystals, followed by the required number of yellow (x), green (y), and blue (z) balls.
2. **Calculating Required Crystals**:
   - **Yellow Crystals**: `2 * x + y` (for yellow and green balls).
   - **Blue Crystals**: `y + 3 * z` (for green and blue balls).
3. **Deficit Calculation**:
   - Compute the additional yellow crystals needed as the maximum of 0 and the difference between required and available yellow crystals.
   - Similarly, compute the additional blue crystals needed.
4. **Output**: The sum of the additional yellow and blue crystals is printed as the result.

This approach efficiently calculates the minimum number of additional crystals needed using straightforward arithmetic operations and ensures correctness by handling all edge cases through the use of `max` functions.
