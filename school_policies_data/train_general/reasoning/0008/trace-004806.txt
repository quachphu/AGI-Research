To solve this problem, we need to determine Takahashi's Inner Rating based on his Displayed Rating and the number of contests he has participated in. The Displayed Rating is calculated differently depending on whether the number of contests is less than 10 or not.

### Approach
1. **Understanding the Problem**: The Displayed Rating is equal to the Inner Rating if the member has participated in 10 or more contests. If the member has participated in fewer than 10 contests, the Displayed Rating is the Inner Rating minus \(100 \times (10 - K)\), where \(K\) is the number of contests participated in.
2. **Formulate the Solution**:
   - If the number of contests (N) is 10 or more, the Inner Rating is the same as the Displayed Rating (R).
   - If N is less than 10, the Inner Rating can be calculated by reversing the formula used to compute the Displayed Rating. Specifically, the Inner Rating is \(R + 100 \times (10 - N)\).

### Solution Code
```python
n, r = map(int, input().split())
if n >= 10:
    print(r)
else:
    print(r + 100 * (10 - n))
```

- **Reading Input**: The input consists of two integers, N (number of contests) and R (Displayed Rating).
- **Condition Check**: 
  - If N is 10 or more, the Inner Rating is directly printed as R.
  - If N is less than 10, the Inner Rating is calculated by adjusting R using the formula \(R + 100 \times (10 - N)\) to account for the deduction applied to the Displayed Rating.

This approach efficiently determines the Inner Rating with a simple conditional check and arithmetic operation, ensuring correctness and optimal performance.
