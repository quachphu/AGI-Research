2. **Summing Over All Lengths**: The total number of valid sequences for lengths from 1 to N is the sum of combinations \(\sum_{k=1}^{N} \binom{M + k - 1}{k}\). 3. **Modular Arithmetic**: Given the constraints, direct computation of large binomial coefficients is infeasible. Instead, we use Lucas's Theorem to compute binomial coefficients modulo a prime number \(10^6 + 3\) efficiently.
