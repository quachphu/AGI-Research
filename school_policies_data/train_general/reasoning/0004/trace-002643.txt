To solve this problem, we need to simulate a game where Alice and Bob take turns eating candies from opposite ends of a row. Alice starts from the left and Bob from the right. Each player must eat candies such that the sum of the candies eaten in their current move is strictly greater than the sum of the candies eaten by the other player in their previous move. The game ends when all candies are eaten.

### Approach
1. **Initialization**: Read the input values and initialize pointers for the leftmost and rightmost candies.
2. **First Move**: Alice starts by eating the first candy from the left. This is a special case handled separately.
3. **Alternating Moves**: After the first move, Bob and Alice take turns. Each player eats candies from their respective end until the sum of the current move exceeds the previous move's sum by the other player.
4. **Termination Check**: After each move, check if there are any candies left. If not, the game ends.
5. **Result Calculation**: Track the total candies eaten by each player and the number of moves made.
