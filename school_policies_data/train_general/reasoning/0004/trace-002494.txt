The solution involves using combinatorial mathematics and modular arithmetic to efficiently compute the result under large constraints.

### Approach
1. **Stirling Numbers of the Second Kind**: These numbers, denoted \( S(k, t) \), count the number of ways to partition \( k \) objects into \( t \) non-empty subsets. They are computed using dynamic programming.
2. **Falling Factorials**: The falling factorial \( (n)_t \) is computed modulo 998244353 to handle large values of \( n \).
3. **Modular Inverse**: Since the probability of drawing the joker is \( 1/m \), we use modular arithmetic to compute the inverse of \( m \) and its powers efficiently.
4. **Summation**: The final result is a summation of terms involving Stirling numbers, falling factorials, and powers of the modular inverse of \( m \).

### Solution Code
```python
mod = 998244353

n, m, k = map(int, input().split())

# Precompute Stirling numbers of the second kind S(k, t)
stir = [[0] * (k+1) for _ in range(k+1)]
stir[0][0] = 1
for i in range(1, k+1):
    for j in range(1, i+1):
        stir[i][j] = (j * stir[i-1][j] + stir[i-1][j-1]) % mod

# Precompute falling factorial (n)_t mod mod
fall = [0] * (k+1)
fall[0] = 1
for t in range(1, k+1):
    term = (n - (t - 1)) % mod
    fall[t] = (fall[t-1] * term) % mod

# Precompute inverse of m and its powers
inv_m = pow(m, mod - 2, mod)
pow_inv = [1] * (k+1)
for t in range(1, k+1):
    pow_inv[t] = (pow_inv[t-1] * inv_m) % mod

# Calculate the sum
max_t = min(k, n)
ans = 0
for t in range(1, max_t + 1):
    s = stir[k][t]
    f = fall[t]
    p = pow_inv[t]
    ans = (ans + s * f % mod * p) % mod

print(ans)
```

3. **Modular Inverse**: The inverse of \( m \) is computed using Fermat's Little Theorem, which states \( m^{mod-2} \mod mod \) gives the modular inverse. Powers of this inverse are precomputed for efficiency.

4. **Summation**: For each \( t \) from 1 to the minimum of \( k \) and \( n \), we compute the term \( S(k, t) \cdot (n)_t \cdot (1/m)^t \) modulo 998244353 and sum these terms to get the final result.
