### Approach
1. **Problem Analysis**:
   - The contests are held in the order ABC, ARC, AGC, and then repeat.
   - ABC is rated for contestants with a rating less than 1200.
   - ARC is rated for contestants with a rating less than 2800.
   - AGC is rated for all contestants regardless of their rating.

2. **Key Insight**:
   - The next contest that Takahashi can participate in is the first one in the sequence (ABC, ARC, AGC) where he meets the rating requirement.
   - If Takahashi's rating is less than 1200, the next contest is ABC.
   - If his rating is between 1200 and 2799 (inclusive), the next contest is ARC.
   - If his rating is 2800 or higher, the next contest is AGC.

3. **Algorithm**:
   - Read the rating input.
   - Check the rating against the thresholds for each contest in sequence (ABC first, then ARC, then AGC).
   - Print the name of the first contest for which Takahashi is eligible.

```python
R = int(input())

    print("ABC")

    print("ARC")
else:
    print("AGC")
```

- **Reading Input**: The input is read as an integer which represents Takahashi's current rating.
- **Checking Conditions**:
  - If the rating is less than 1200, the next contest is ABC.
  - If the rating is 1200 or higher but less than 2800, the next contest is ARC.
  - If the rating is 2800 or higher, the next contest is AGC.
- **Output**: Based on the conditions, the appropriate contest name is printed.

This approach efficiently checks the eligibility criteria in sequence and ensures the correct contest is selected based on Takahashi's rating, providing an optimal solution with a time complexity of O(1).
