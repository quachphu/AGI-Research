### Approach
1. **Understanding the Structure**: Each floor of the house consists of rooms and a ceiling. Each room requires 2 cards, and each ceiling between adjacent rooms requires 1 card. The total number of cards for a floor with `k` rooms is given by the formula `3k - 1`.

2. **Formulating the Problem**: For a house with `h` floors, the total number of cards required is the sum of the cards required for each floor. This sum must exactly equal `n`. We need to find all valid values of `h` such that there exists a sequence of rooms per floor that satisfies the structural constraints.

3. **Mathematical Conditions**:
   - The sum of cards for `h` floors must be `n`.
   - The number of rooms on each subsequent floor must be strictly less than the previous one.
   - We derive the conditions for valid heights by solving quadratic equations and checking divisibility.

4. **Efficient Calculation**: Using mathematical insights, we determine the maximum possible height `h` and check for valid heights using modular arithmetic to ensure efficient computation.

import math

n = int(input())

        count += 1
