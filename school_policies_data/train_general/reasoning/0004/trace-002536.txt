### Approach

The formula for the number of non-decreasing sequences of length N with elements from 1 to N is given by the binomial coefficient \( C(2N - 1, N) \). This formula is derived from the concept of combinations with repetition, where we need to choose N elements from a set of N elements allowing repetitions, which can be mathematically represented as \( C(2N - 1, N) \).
