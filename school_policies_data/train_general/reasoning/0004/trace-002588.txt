    # Compute for each frame the top-right point.

    right_bottom_y = max(start_frame[1], start_frame[3])

To solve this problem, we need to determine the reading order of frames in a comic based on specific rules. The frames are axis-aligned rectangles, and the order is determined by selecting the rightmost and topmost frame first, then recursively processing frames above a horizontal line drawn from the bottom-right corner of the selected frame.

### Approach
1. **Understanding the Coordinate System**: The x-axis increases to the left, and the y-axis increases downward. The rightmost frame is the one with the smallest x-coordinate for its top-right corner.
2. **Selecting the Starting Frame**: In each step, select the frame with the smallest x-coordinate (rightmost) and, if there are ties, the smallest y-coordinate (topmost).
3. **Drawing the Line**: From the bottom-right corner of the selected frame, draw a horizontal line leftward until it touches another frame's right edge or the paper's edge.
4. **Partitioning Frames**: Frames above the line (including those touching the line) are processed next, followed by the remaining frames.
