To solve this problem, we need to determine Pooja's account balance after she attempts to withdraw a certain amount of money from an ATM. The solution must check if the withdrawal amount is a multiple of 5 and if there are sufficient funds in the account, including a bank charge of 0.50 USD for each successful transaction. 

### Approach
1. **Input Reading**: Read the withdrawal amount and the initial account balance from the standard input.
2. **Check Withdrawal Conditions**:
   - **Multiple of 5**: Verify if the withdrawal amount is a multiple of 5.
   - **Sufficient Funds**: Ensure the account balance can cover the withdrawal amount plus the bank charge (0.50 USD).
3. **Update Balance**: If both conditions are satisfied, deduct the withdrawal amount and the bank charge from the account balance. If not, retain the original balance.
4. **Output**: Print the resulting balance formatted to two decimal places.

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    inputs = input().split()
    x = int(inputs[0])
    y = float(inputs[1])
    
    if x % 5 != 0:
        print("{:.2f}".format(y))
    else:
        total = x + 0.5
        if total > y:
            print("{:.2f}".format(y))
        else:
            print("{:.2f}".format(y - total))

main()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The input is read as a string and split into two parts. The first part is converted to an integer (withdrawal amount), and the second part is converted to a float (initial balance).
2. **Check Multiple of 5**: If the withdrawal amount is not a multiple of 5, the transaction is declined immediately, and the initial balance is printed.
3. **Check Sufficient Funds**: If the withdrawal amount is a valid multiple of 5, the total amount (withdrawal + bank charge) is checked against the account balance. If insufficient, the initial balance is printed.
4. **Update Balance**: If both checks pass, the total amount is deducted from the balance, and the new balance is printed with two decimal places.

This approach ensures that all constraints are checked efficiently, and the solution is both optimal and easy to understand.
