To solve this problem, we need to calculate the total amount Mr. Takaha will pay for N items, where he can use a discount coupon to get the highest-priced item for half price. The remaining items are purchased at their regular prices.

### Approach
1. **Input Reading**: Read the number of items, N, followed by the prices of each item.
2. **Sum Calculation**: Compute the total sum of all item prices.
3. **Maximum Price Identification**: Determine the highest price among the items.
4. **Discount Application**: Subtract half of the highest price from the total sum (since the highest-priced item is only charged at half its regular price).

### Solution Code
```python
n = int(input())
prices = [int(input()) for _ in range(n)]
total = sum(prices) - (max(prices) // 2)
print(total)
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The first input line gives the number of items, N. The subsequent N lines provide the prices of each item.
2. **Summing Prices**: We calculate the sum of all item prices as if they were all bought at full price.
3. **Finding Maximum Price**: Identify the highest price in the list of item prices.
4. **Applying Discount**: The highest price is halved and subtracted from the total sum. This adjustment is valid because the original sum includes the full price of the highest-priced item, which we replace with half its price using the discount coupon.

This approach efficiently computes the total cost by leveraging basic arithmetic operations and list functions, ensuring an optimal and straightforward solution.
