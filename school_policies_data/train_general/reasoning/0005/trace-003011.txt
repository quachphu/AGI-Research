2. **Converted Groups**: Calculate the maximum number of groups that can be formed by converting `c` pieces into `S` pieces. This involves using both the original `S` pieces and the converted ones, considering the total number of `c` pieces available for both conversion and group formation.
3. **Optimal Solution**: The optimal number of groups is the maximum of the direct groups and the converted groups, ensuring we do not exceed the constraints imposed by the available pieces.

n, m = map(int, input().split())
