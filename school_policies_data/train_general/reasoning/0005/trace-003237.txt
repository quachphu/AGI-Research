The problem involves a complete graph where the energy at each vertex is updated based on a linear transformation involving the Hamming distance between vertices. Given the constraints, a direct simulation is infeasible, so we use the Fast Walsh-Hadamard Transform (FWHT) to efficiently compute the result.

### Approach
1. **Understanding the Transformation**: The energy update at each vertex is a linear combination of energies from all other vertices, weighted by coefficients based on the Hamming distance (number of differing bits) between vertices. This can be viewed as a convolution in the XOR domain.
2. **Fast Walsh-Hadamard Transform (FWHT)**: This transform allows us to convert the problem into the frequency domain where convolutions become element-wise multiplications. This reduces the complexity from O(n²) to O(n log n).
3. **Matrix Exponentiation**: Using FWHT, we transform the initial energy distribution and the transformation coefficients. After raising the transformed coefficients to the power `t`, we perform an inverse FWHT to get the result.
