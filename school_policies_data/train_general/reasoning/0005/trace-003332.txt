To solve this problem, we need to maximize the total pleasure DZY can get after performing exactly k operations on an n×m matrix. Each operation can either be a row operation or a column operation, and each operation's pleasure is determined by the sum of the elements in the chosen row or column before the operation.

### Approach
1. **Problem Analysis**:
   - Each row operation reduces every element in the row by p and adds the sum of the row before the reduction to the total pleasure.
   - Each column operation reduces every element in the column by p and adds the sum of the column before the reduction to the total pleasure.
   - The challenge is to determine the optimal sequence of operations to maximize the total pleasure.

2. **Key Insights**:
   - **Greedy Approach**: Use max-heaps to always pick the row or column with the highest current sum for the next operation.
   - **Precomputation**: Precompute the maximum possible pleasure for all possible numbers of row operations (0 to k) and column operations (0 to k) using max-heaps.
   - **Interdependency Adjustment**: Adjust the total pleasure by considering the interdependency between row and column operations. The total pleasure from x row operations and y column operations (where x + y = k) is given by `sum_row[x] + sum_col[y] - x*y*p`, where the term `x*y*p` accounts for the interdependency effect.
