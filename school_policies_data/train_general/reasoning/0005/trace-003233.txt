To solve this problem, we need to determine the ratio of the empty screen area to the total screen area after scaling a movie to fit within a monitor while preserving its aspect ratio. The solution involves comparing the aspect ratios of the monitor and the movie to determine the optimal scaling factor and then calculating the resulting empty area.

### Approach
1. **Understand Aspect Ratios**: The monitor's aspect ratio is given as `a:b`, and the movie's aspect ratio is `c:d`. We need to determine how the movie fits into the monitor while preserving its aspect ratio.
2. **Compare Products of Ratios**: Calculate the products `b*c` (monitor's height multiplied by movie's width) and `a*d` (monitor's width multiplied by movie's height). These products help determine which dimension (width or height) limits the scaling.
3. **Determine Scaling Factor**: The limiting dimension is determined by comparing `b*c` and `a*d`. The movie will be scaled to fit the dimension that results in the smaller product, ensuring the movie fits within the monitor.
4. **Calculate Empty Area Ratio**: The empty area ratio is derived from the difference between the larger product and the smaller product, divided by the larger product. This fraction is then simplified to its irreducible form.

### Solution Code
```python
import math

a, b, c, d = map(int, input().split())
bc = b * c
ad = a * d
numerator = abs(bc - ad)
denominator = max(bc, ad)
g = math.gcd(numerator, denominator)
p = numerator // g
q = denominator // g
print(f"{p}/{q}")
```

### Explanation
1. **Input Reading**: The input values `a`, `b`, `c`, and `d` are read and parsed.
2. **Product Calculation**: Compute `bc` (product of monitor's height and movie's width) and `ad` (product of monitor's width and movie's height).
3. **Numerator and Denominator**: Calculate the absolute difference between `bc` and `ad` as the numerator and the maximum of `bc` and `ad` as the denominator.
4. **Simplify Fraction**: Use the greatest common divisor (GCD) to reduce the fraction formed by the numerator and denominator to its simplest form.
5. **Output**: Print the simplified fraction representing the empty screen area ratio.

This approach efficiently determines the optimal scaling and computes the required ratio using basic arithmetic and the GCD function for simplification, ensuring accuracy and performance.
