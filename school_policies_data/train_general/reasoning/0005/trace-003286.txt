### Approach
1. **Precompute Minimum Exchange Rates**: For each day, compute the minimum exchange rates for dollars and pounds up to that day. Track both the minimum rate and the earliest day this rate occurs.
2. **Binary Search for Earliest Day**: Use binary search to determine the earliest day `d` where Nura can buy `k` gadgets. For each candidate day `d`, compute the minimum cost for each gadget using the precomputed minimum exchange rates.
3. **Check Feasibility**: For each candidate day, sort the gadgets by their minimum cost and check if the sum of the `k` smallest costs is within the budget `s`.
4. **Reconstruct Solution**: Once the earliest feasible day is found, determine which gadgets to buy and their respective purchase days based on the precomputed minimum exchange rates.
