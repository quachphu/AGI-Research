To solve this problem, we need to determine the maximum total score JOI can achieve by selecting 3 out of 4 science subjects and 1 out of 2 social subjects. The goal is to maximize the sum of the selected scores.

### Approach
1. **Input Reading**: Read the scores for the six subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Earth Science, History, Geography).
2. **Generate Science Combinations**: Calculate the sum of all possible combinations of 3 out of the 4 science subjects. This results in four possible sums.
3. **Determine Maximum Social Score**: Compute the maximum score between History and Geography.
4. **Calculate Maximum Total Score**: Find the maximum sum from the science combinations and add the maximum social score to it.

### Solution Code
```python
a = int(input())
b = int(input())
c = int(input())
d = int(input())
e = int(input())
f = int(input())

sum1 = a + b + c
sum2 = a + b + d
sum3 = a + c + d
sum4 = b + c + d

max_science = max(sum1, sum2, sum3, sum4)
max_social = max(e, f)

print(max_science + max_social)
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Inputs**: The scores for each subject are read from standard input.
2. **Science Combinations**: The four possible sums of three science subjects are calculated by excluding each of the four subjects one at a time.
3. **Social Score**: The higher score between History (E) and Geography (F) is determined.
4. **Max Total Calculation**: The highest sum from the science combinations is added to the highest social score to get the maximum possible total score.

This approach ensures that we efficiently compute the maximum possible score by considering all valid combinations and selecting the optimal ones.
