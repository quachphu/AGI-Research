To solve this problem, we need to determine the winner of a game of One Card Poker between Alice and Bob based on the strength of their cards. The card strengths follow a specific order where 1 is the strongest card, followed by 13, 12, ..., down to 2.

### Approach
1. **Understand Card Strengths**: The key insight is that card 1 (Ace) is the strongest, followed by cards 13, 12, ..., 2 in descending order. We can model this by treating the strength of card 1 as 14, and the strength of other cards as their face value.
2. **Compare Strengths**: Convert each player's card to its corresponding strength value. Compare these strengths to determine the winner:
   - If Alice's strength is higher, she wins.
   - If Bob's strength is higher, he wins.
   - If both strengths are equal, the game is a draw.

### Solution Code
```python
def main():
    a, b = map(int, input().split())
    strength_a = 14 if a == 1 else a
    strength_b = 14 if b == 1 else b
    if strength_a > strength_b:
        print("Alice")
    elif strength_a < strength_b:
        print("Bob")
    else:
        print("Draw")

main()
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The input consists of two integers, A and B, representing the cards of Alice and Bob respectively.
2. **Strength Conversion**: Convert each card to its strength value. If the card is 1, its strength is 14; otherwise, it's the card's face value.
3. **Comparison**: Compare the converted strengths of Alice and Bob. Print "Alice" if her strength is higher, "Bob" if his strength is higher, and "Draw" if they are equal.

This approach efficiently converts and compares the card strengths using straightforward conditional checks, ensuring an optimal solution with constant time complexity.
