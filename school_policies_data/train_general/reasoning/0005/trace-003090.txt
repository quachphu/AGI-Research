To solve this problem, we need to determine the optimal sequence of bomb drops that ensures all tanks on a 1×n grid are destroyed with the minimum number of bombs. The key insight is to account for the movement of tanks when they are hit for the first time, ensuring that every possible initial position and subsequent movement is covered.

### Approach
The optimal strategy involves three main steps:
1. **Bomb all even positions**: This covers tanks in even positions, forcing them to move to neighboring odd positions.
2. **Bomb all odd positions**: This covers both the original tanks in odd positions and the tanks that moved from even positions to odd positions after the first bombing.
3. **Bomb all even positions again**: This covers tanks that moved from odd positions to even positions after the second bombing.

By following this sequence, we ensure that all tanks are hit twice, either directly in their initial positions or after moving, thus destroying them all.

### Solution Code
```python
n = int(input())
even = list(range(2, n + 1, 2))
odd = list(range(1, n + 1, 2))
sequence = even + odd + even
print(len(sequence))
print(' '.join(map(str, sequence)))
```

### Explanation
1. **Even Positions First**: By bombing all even positions first (2, 4, 6, ...), any tanks in these positions are hit and move to adjacent odd positions (either left or right).
2. **Odd Positions Next**: Bombing all odd positions (1, 3, 5, ...) next ensures that both the original tanks in odd positions and those that moved from even positions are hit. If a tank was hit in an even position, it moves to an odd position, which is then bombed in this step.
3. **Even Positions Again**: Finally, bombing the even positions again catches any tanks that moved from odd positions to even positions after the second step.

This approach guarantees that every tank is hit twice, regardless of their initial positions and movements, using the minimum number of bombs. The sequence generated ensures all possibilities are covered efficiently.
