### Approach

2. **Ray Casting Algorithm**: If the point is not on any edge, use the ray casting algorithm to determine if the point is inside or outside the polygon. This algorithm works by counting how many times a horizontal ray from the point intersects the polygon's edges. If the count is odd, the point is inside; otherwise, it is outside.
