To solve this problem, we need to determine if there exists an integer Z that satisfies specific conditions set by two empires, which would prevent a war. The conditions are based on the coordinates of their capitals and the cities they want to control.

### Approach
1. **Understand the Conditions**: The integer Z must satisfy:
   - It lies between the coordinates of the capitals of Empire A (X) and Empire B (Y), i.e., X < Z ≤ Y.
   - All cities under Empire A must be to the left of Z, i.e., all cities in A must be less than Z.
   - All cities under Empire B must be to the right of or at Z, i.e., all cities in B must be greater than or equal to Z.

2. **Key Observations**:
   - The maximum coordinate of cities under Empire A (max_x) must be less than Z.
   - The minimum coordinate of cities under Empire B (min_y) must be greater than or equal to Z.
   - Z must be an integer within the interval (X, Y].

3. **Determine Valid Range for Z**:
   - The lower bound for Z is the maximum of max_x and X.
   - The upper bound for Z is the minimum of min_y and Y.
   - If the lower bound is less than the upper bound, there exists at least one valid Z, otherwise, war is inevitable.

### Solution Code
```python
n, m, X, Y = map(int, input().split())
x = list(map(int, input().split()))
y = list(map(int, input().split()))

max_x = max(x)
min_y = min(y)

lower = max(max_x, X)
upper = min(min_y, Y)

print("No War" if lower < upper else "War")
```

### Explanation
1. **Reading Input**: The input consists of the number of cities for each empire, the coordinates of their capitals, and the coordinates of the cities they want to control.
2. **Calculate Bounds**:
   - `max_x` is the maximum coordinate of cities under Empire A, ensuring all these cities are left of Z.
   - `min_y` is the minimum coordinate of cities under Empire B, ensuring all these cities are right of or at Z.
3. **Determine Valid Range**:
   - `lower` is the highest value between `max_x` and X, ensuring Z is greater than both.
   - `upper` is the smallest value between `min_y` and Y, ensuring Z is within the capital's range and satisfies Empire B's cities.
4. **Check Validity**: If `lower` is less than `upper`, there exists a valid Z, otherwise, war is declared.

This approach efficiently checks the conditions using basic arithmetic and comparison operations, ensuring optimal performance given the constraints.
