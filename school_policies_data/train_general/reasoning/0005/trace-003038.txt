To solve this problem, we need to simulate the process of children visiting a dentist, where each child's crying affects the confidence of subsequent children in the queue. The goal is to determine the order in which children successfully get their teeth treated, considering chain reactions caused by their crying.

### Approach
1. **Initialization**: Read the input values and store each child's information, including their cry volume, hall cry volume, and initial confidence.
2. **Processing the Queue**: For each child in the queue:
   - Treat the child and record their visit.
   - Apply the cry volume effect to the next children in the queue.
   - Check for any children whose confidence drops below zero due to the cry volume, handle their exit, and apply their hall cry volume to subsequent children, which might trigger a chain reaction.
3. **Chain Reaction Handling**: After each child's treatment, repeatedly check and remove children whose confidence drops below zero, applying their hall cry volume to subsequent children until no more children are affected.
