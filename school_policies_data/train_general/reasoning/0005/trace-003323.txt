3. **Priority Queue (Dijkstra's Algorithm)**: Use a modified Dijkstra's algorithm to find the shortest path. The priority queue (min-heap) will store tuples representing the current cost, city, parity (even or odd), and the weight of the first edge in the current pair (if applicable).
4. **Distance Arrays**: Maintain two distance arrays:
   - `dist0` for the minimum cost to reach each city with an even number of edges.
   - `dist1` for the minimum cost to reach each city with an odd number of edges, along with the weight of the first edge in the current pair.

def main():

                    heapq.heappush(heap, (new_cost, v, 0, -1))

        else:
            res = dist0[t]

                output.append('-1')
            else:
                output.append(str(res))
    print(' '.join(output))
